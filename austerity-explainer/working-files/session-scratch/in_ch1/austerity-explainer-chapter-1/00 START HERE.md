# Start here

**What this is:** everything behind chapter 1 of "Where austerity came from" (the paper pop-up film,
3 minutes 41 seconds): the research, the notes and decisions, the scripts, Malina's recordings and
timings, the tools and drawing code, the painting prompts, and the rules for voice, tone and writing
style. Made on 12 October 2026. Source for the story: Clara E. Mattei, *The Capital Order* (2022).

## Read these first

1. `01 Rules - voice, tone and writing style.md`: how Malina is recorded, the tone of the narration,
   and the writing style from your Pinker toolkit, in one place.
2. `project-notes/Austerity explainer - project story.md`: the whole story of the project, with a
   word list and the numbered log.
3. `project-notes/Decisions.md`: every decision, in your own words.

## What is not inside, and where it is

- The finished film, captions and cover: sent earlier as the "13 ..." files.
- The 53 paintings and their cut-out masks: in the three "14 Paper pop-up film kit" zips (part 2 and
  part 3), which also hold the tools made ready for a new series.
- The full-length sound files and the full-quality master film (hundreds of megabytes): they stay in
  the project and can be rebuilt from what is here plus the paintings.

## Traps

- The drawing code expects to sit in a project folder with its libraries installed and the paper
  pieces made from the paintings; to run it, use the kit (its `00 START HERE.md` walks through it).
- The soundtrack is yours: use it for your own films only.
- Your words in `Decisions.md` are quoted exactly, spelling included. Don't tidy them.
