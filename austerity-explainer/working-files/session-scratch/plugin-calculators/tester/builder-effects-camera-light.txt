**1. Effects planner (plan_effects.py)**
- **Wind:** it finds the level direction most across every view that sees a chimney, traces both signs with the same sums the page uses for chimney smoke, and keeps the sign that passes in every view.
- **Plumes:** for each plume and view it gives how far the plume travels while seen (at least 0.15 of the width). It also checks the puffs' top edges against word cards (the skeptic's test), whether the plume fades before another factory's chimney, and thick smoke over a figure. It drops smoke that never enters the frame, or whose chimney is out of the frame where the shot holds.
- **Other commands:** plume colour against the sky on the plate, the mouth against the chimney top, contact shadows from a prop's measured base, the 15-level window-change check, the glow pulse and flame variety, and a check that paper pages make every effect through kit.effect.

**2. Camera audit (camera_audit.py, extended)**
- The old audit is unchanged. On old chapter 2's logs its reports match the old tool word for word: 9 problems before the camera rule; 21 shots and 0 problems after.
- **--timeline:** works out the page's window. It equals the stage's own page edges.
- **--built-set:** for every move it checks:
  - no turn;
  - where the street's lines meet does not drift;
  - magnification at the nearest painted thing, reading the place's accepted list;
  - the frame stays 0.03 inside the painted plate, held frames included.

**3. Light planner (plan_lights.py)**
- **Plan, light by light:** role and the few lights that cast shadows, starting watts by the inverse square from the subject's own light, reach from Blender ray hits, the cones that cover the subject, and links (its housing; the props round a fire).
- **Plan, frame and subject:** lamps landing on the subject or where the lines meet, a light behind the camera flooding the near ground, a lamp lighting the face it hangs on, the subject's total light against its key (burn-out risk), and a dark-subject plan.
- **Other commands:** contrast (measured the people test's way), exposure per view, and a render-log check that refuses a picture with a shadow fault and reads each plate's drawing time.
- The tuning loop (tune_lights) is left for later.
- A small Blender helper, set_measurements.py, measures prop bases, everything a light's cone meets, and where the set's lamps are. It only reads the .blend files.

##### api
**plan_effects.py** (all commands take --json, --out, --report and --settings; exit 1 when a problem stands)
- `wind --place-facts F [--smoke page.js|smoke.json] [--smoking NAME=KIND ...] [--view V ...] [--main-view V] [--card VIEW:NAME=l,t,r,b ...] [--wind=x,y,z] [--full-trace]`
- `colour --place-facts F --view V --picture PLATE [--spot plate|composed] [--smoke ...] [--smoking ...] [--wind=...]`
- `contact-shadows --place-facts F [--props a,b] [--footprints F.json] [--slide-start T]`
- `window-change --box l,t,r,b STILL_AT_WORD STILL_LATER...`
- `fire [--glow BASE,AMP] [--page-file P.js ...] [--effects-file pit-village-effects.js]`
- `lint PAGE.js...`
- Functions:
  - reading settings: read_smoke(settings, path), read_smoke_from_page_code(path)
  - tracing: puff_at(kind, age), trace_plume(camera, top, kind, wind_three, step), trace_fire_smoke(camera, point, fire, step, direction)
  - judging: judge_plume(samples, settings, cards, others, figures, emitter_across), candidate_winds(...), should_drop(entered, mouth_at_held_spot)
  - the rest: window_change(box, first, others, settings), judge_window_change(largest, settings), read_glow_pulse(path), read_flames(path), lint_page(path), resting_surface(...)

**plan_lights.py**
- `plan --place-facts F --view V [--state S] [--reference LIGHT] [--role LIGHT=ROLE] [--beam-hits LIGHT=beam.json] [--surfaces F.json] [--lamps lamps.json] [--plate-width N]`
- `exposure --mean V=LEVELS ... | --picture V=FILE ...`
- `contrast --place-facts F --view V --picture P [--subject-mask solo.png | --subject-box-3d cx,cy,cz,w,d,h] [--spot] [--frame-height 1920]`
- `render-log LOG...` (exit 1 when a log shows a shadow fault)
- Functions: reach_from_beam(beam, targets, excluded, casts_shadow, settings), beam_from_boxes, cones_for, housing_of, nearest_lit_surface, surfaces_check, lamps_check, subject_light_balance, saved_pictures(log text).

**camera_audit.py**
- The old form is unchanged: `python3 -I camera_audit.py CAMERA.json [--page N] [--window FROM TO]`.
- New: `--timeline timeline.json --page N [--page-files DIR]`.
- New: `--built-set [--place-facts F --view V] [--shot TEXT] [--street x,y,z] [--projector camera.json] [--painted NAME=x,y,z ...] [--nearest M] [--floor Z] [--painting-height ROWS]`, plus --json, --out and --settings.
- Exit 1 on any problem.
- Functions: find_shots(data, page, window) (what audit() prints), page_window(timeline, n, settings, page_files), check_built_set(found, place, settings).
- The six old constants stay in the file, so the core's drift test still guards them.

**set_measurements.py** (Blender, read only)
- `blender -b SET.blend --python set_measurements.py -- footprints|beam|objects ...`
- footprints: --check F --groups a,b [--slice 0.05] --out F
- beam: --check F (--at x,y,z --aim x,y,z --cone DEG | --light NAME) [--rings --per-ring --far] --out F
- objects: --match PATTERN --out F
