# Style note: canal lock side (22 October 2026)

PROVISIONAL: no reference picture exists, so the numbers come from the first honest picture (not yet drawn). Written
from the plugin's template by the verifier, who is a newcomer to the plugin.

## 0. THE RULE FOR EVERY BUILDER
The film owner's rule, word for word: "These things need to be integrated into the style. So it's just part of design."
Wear, moss, cracks, stains, chips, weeds are designed elements in this place's own language: a few big, clean, simple
shapes, not photographic noise. A moss patch is one soft rounded shape; a crack is one clean dark line.

## 1. Form
Chunky, simple, solid. Brick walls are big flat faces with clear courses; coping stones are fat slabs with a visible
joint; timber gate leaves are thick frames with a few heavy planks. Bevels 1 to 3 cm. Silhouettes readable when squinted:
wall, towpath edge, the V of the gate, the two long balance beams, the steps.

## 2. Edges and surfaces
Crisp edges, no grain or lens dirt. Brick: mostly one warm colour with soft large variation and a few clear marks (a
cracked brick, a spalled patch, a dark water stain band at the waterline). Timber: silvered grey with a few clear
splits. Gravel: soft, small, with a worn lighter strip down the middle (horse-trodden).

## 3. Palette
Brick warm red-brown #8a4b38, #7a3f2f, #a65f45; stone coping pale grey #a39d93; timber silver-grey #7d7a72 and dark
oak #4d4034; gravel #9b9185; water dark green-brown #3d4a3f; moss #5e7a3a; rust #8a4a22; accent: none (a muted place).

## 4. Value range and light
Soft overcast daylight from the upper left, cool shadows, long soft shadows. Detail sits inside that light.

## 5. Detail per region
One readable feature per 20 to 30 cm on the towpath, 50 cm on the wall, 5 to 10 cm on timber. Fine contrast
(detail energy) target 3 to 9; above 10 is too photographic.

## 6. What this style does NOT have
Photographic grain, hair-level grass, pores, sharp dust glints, lens flares, people, text, modern objects (no plastic,
no cars, no signs).

## 7. Checklist
0. Designed-in test. 1. Squint test. 2. Palette. 3. Detail energy. 4. Light. 5. One story per hand-span.
