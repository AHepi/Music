ITEM BY ITEM
(1) Moss and mould on the planks: FIXED. The bridge base is greyer: average plank brightness 0.425 before, 0.377 now. The walking line is lighter than the plank average by 0.049 (target 0.02 to 0.06). The deck stays warm and has little moss on purpose. Posts are greyer and darker, with rain streaks and a moss band at the foot. A few cool damp stains were added, but I did not measure them separately.
(2) Same knot on most planks: FIXED. The grain picture no longer has knots. Each plank now gets its own knot (4 knot shapes, own place and size), and 31 of 59 bridge planks (52 percent) have one. Half the planks have the grain turned round by 180 degrees. The grain picture is now a 2 m x 0.5 m mirror tile, so every plank takes a different random window, mirrored or turned. The integrator's rule that the Normal Map node reads its picture directly still holds: the knots are a separate 16-bit height picture applied with a Bump node.
(3) Open outer corner and dark triangle at the elbows: FIXED. 8 short oblique offcut planks (4 per turn) now fill the outer corner of both turns, 4 short corner rails carry them, and the diagonal breaker boards are now as long as the mitre line (1.24 m and 1.34 m instead of 1.20 m). Looking down, the corners are closed. The bridge outline overlaps today's outline by 0.919 (the builder had 0.869; the limit is 0.85).
(4) Pegs: FIXED. Bridge pegs 7 mm to 10.5 mm radius and height (1.5 times), deck pegs and post pegs the same, nail heads 1.5 times. I also made my maple leaves on the bridge and deck about 1.4 times bigger. I tried a dark ring round each peg; it rendered pale and looked like a hat brim, so I took it out.
(5) Footing stones: FIXED. They now stand 1 to 3 cm out of the water (tops at z -0.107 to -0.090), 40 to 53 cm wide, darker and with a dry top, a dark wet rim and soft moss. Under each post pair they read as stone pads in the hero and the close-ups. They are now chunky blocks (33 to 36 cm tall) instead of flat slabs under the water.
(6) Moss at the foot of the veranda steps: PARTLY. I added 8 small moss cushions at the foot of the lower step and a few groups where the two steps meet. In the combined scene the ground builder's big moss cushions already cover that foot, so mine are mostly hidden there. I also took the neon-green line pattern off the step stone (see failures and notes).
Not done: half-lap joints and cut notches (they would not be visible at any camera).

Cavity picture (groove darkening) is the same mirror tile. End grain, post timber and footing stone normal maps are unchanged; the six CT stone materials of the steps and piers keep their normal maps. ######## FIXER roof
-- summary
All four of my items are fixed, and the fixed stage ran clean on RunPod. Rocks are calm, the lantern moss is no longer a bright sticker, the roof has moss cushions and a calmer glaze, and the wall cracks are bolder. I changed only my own roof, walls, stones and lantern parts. I changed no existing node group, lights or world, and never wrote in another owner's folder or saved over the start scene.

(1) ROCKS: fixed. The stripes and neon-green lines were not the stone colour scans. Drawing the weathering kit's colour step by step on RunPod showed three kit layers: the foot splash and tide band (the wavy "agate" stripes), the wall-crack lines (the diamond lines) and the wall-growth layer (the green band). The kit reads its strengths from custom properties on each object. So on the 5 rocks, 15 pond-edge stones, shore stones, basin, drain pebbles and lantern parts I multiplied those numbers (foot 0.35, splash 0.4, tide green 0.3, cracks 0.33, algae 0.35, growth 0.75). The original values are remembered and put back by the stage's clean-up. I also added soft value blocks (big planes of slightly lighter and darker stone) to the stone material and made the rock cracks a little wider (0.9 to 1.4 cm). The moss caps on the big rocks are untouched.

(2) LANTERN MOSS: fixed. My own roof stage made the sticker. It is replaced by its own darker olive material and seven small pads. Two lie on the cap's hip edges, one at the finial foot, two at the post foot and two in the gallery joint. The outlines are smoothed so they are soft and round.
- The largest pad is 43 percent smaller (0.052 to 0.030 m2). The cap's moss total is 39 percent smaller (0.084 to 0.051 m2).
- Moss pixels on the cap are 27 percent fewer and 21 percent darker, measured in the cap close-up (before 8130 px at luminance 0.193; after 5910 px at 0.152; that run used the previous pad sizes).
- The kit's green patch on the lantern post and finial is calmed with the same dial trick.
- The glow is unchanged (1.0/0.46/0.14, strength 7.0).

(3) ROOF: fixed, with the caveats below.
- Moss cushions: there are now 15 real geometry cushions. They are clusters of round lumps with a rolled edge and a darker rim: 6 at the eave, 4 at the ridge foot and 5 on the ridge cap. They are in the hero view and sit on the roof surface. Mid-slope colour speckles are fewer (probability 0.19 to 0.10) and lichen flecks are fewer and bigger.
- Drip stains: they are 50 percent longer and 90 percent wider, and the eave disc under each of the 11 stains is now dark too. The same tiles carry them, no more of them.
- Slipped tiles: they slip 50 percent further (3 to 7.5 cm, turned 3 to 6 degrees). The same 5 tiles are used.
- Glaze: the crumpled, faceted look is calmed. The tiles are now smooth-shaded across their arch, with edges steeper than 40 degrees kept crisp. The glaze normal map has 3 craze lines instead of 14 and 4 dents instead of 8, at strength 0.55 instead of 0.9 (mean slope 0.065 instead of 0.10). The old scan bump is 0.14 instead of 0.22.
- Extra find: the pale mint slivers between the eave discs were the roof's timber pieces turned mint by the kit's growth layer. They now use a copy of the timber material without that layer (dark wood). Roof leaves are also 45 percent bigger.

(4) WALL CRACKS: fixed. There are fewer cracks (5/5/8 became 4/4/5) and each is twice as wide (1.2 to 2.4 cm) and darker. They are jagged single lines that do not cross each other, and two start where the hero camera looks. I kept at least 4 per long wall so the direction test still passes.

stage_final.py is unchanged: the patch-softening step still finds its nodes. Four things were not done or not changed:
- The 7 pond-edge stones with mirrored UV faces (the builder's known fault) are still there.
- The reviewer reading test (12 of 16) was not done.
- The whole-picture squint test cannot be judged here. The stages are mostly unchanged, so my own difference is tiny; the maple and pond are the other fixers'.
- Slipped tiles and drip tongues are barely readable at 1200 px, because they cover few pixels. The dark discs read best (see failures).
