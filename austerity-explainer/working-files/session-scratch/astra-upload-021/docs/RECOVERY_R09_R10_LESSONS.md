## 1. Design material-specific planes and repair mapping without noise

Use deliberately unequal form-following planes and quiet material-specific marks; flat shading or uniform noise cannot substitute for designed shape.

**Observed scope:** G2 selects seven unequal arch stones, broad piers and two upper surrounds on 21 existing owners of Terrace_1_8 only; whole facade mass and fine brick grid remain unchanged.

- Keep the original painted/chiseled direction: edges and flat surfaces approximate curves; vary plane size, direction, spacing and staggered cuts. Individual quads/triangles are allowed, but uniform tessellation, random triangulation, sponge mottling and all-over noise do not supply design.
- Choose broad planes by material and function: masonry blocks/reveals, cloth folds/tension, wood grain/fibres and metal construction/wear need distinct organizations. These non-masonry examples are proposed design heuristics, not newly qualified R09 implementations.
- G2 replaces the eleven-stone rhythm with seven unequal arch stones and selective pier/surround turns. Long pier fronts deliberately stay calm; do not cut every face merely to demonstrate irregularity.
- G1 stretched limestone stripes came from nearest-surface attribute transfer on changed topology. G2 retained exact geometry and reapplied the inherited 1.05 m face-plane UV rule. Repair the demonstrated mapping cause without adding noise or relighting.
- Check native plus neutral, close plus alternate and hero. The selected G2 gain is local; the small brick grid still dominates the unchanged main wall.

**Coordinate/ownership contract:** Named construction/material assembly; G2 one doorway/two upper surrounds; Rest-face mapping for rebuilt masonry; preserve genuine support/opening geometry; Recheck effective material binding and rest mapping after topology changes; protect accepted figure/barrels.

**Not established:** No whole-building enlargement or validation of every material family. G1 stripes are an attributed UV-transfer defect, distinct from R10 effective OBJECT-slot binding.

## 4. Separate source, contact, history, receiver and maintenance

A used surface needs an inspectable causal story; present supply, old deposits, contacts, receiver suitability and class-specific maintenance are distinct dependencies.

**Observed scope:** Final C5 is a bounded historical-suitability/damp prototype; factory is one contact-history/raster prototype. Research catalogs are proposals, not executed physics qualification.

- Distinguish current wet film, standing-water depth and old moisture/ecological history. C5 current seepage follows source world-Y only; fixed X/Z receiver bands and historical habitat do not move with it.
- Proximity, ambient occlusion, a low pixel or a dark shader patch is not drainage. A bounded terrain-potential/connectivity assessment is not rainfall capacity, finite-volume storage, sediment transport or hydrological simulation.
- Contact transfer requires material/load and an actual eligible receiver hit; clean contact may polish, mechanical abrasion may remain when transferred oil is zero. Zero contact removes new contact classes; chronology controls what survives maintenance.
- Use class-aware ordered cleaning: deposit then clean differs from clean then deposit; removing residue does not automatically erase abrasion. Frozen deposits need not vanish when a current source turns off.
- C5 contains damp/algal base/gutter staining, sparse low cushions and supported fragments. No valid weeds, no new standing-water geometry and no route-derived dirt tracks were emitted there. Inherited R05 wet regions remain authored baseline art.

**Coordinate/ownership contract:** Explicit source, event/contact, receiver and maintenance identities; Stable receiver-local deposit coordinates; independent current supply and historical suitability fields; Choose frozen versus regenerated history explicitly; new source positions do not silently reinterpret past deposits.

**Not established:** No calibrated ecology/species/chronological maintenance simulation, instant moss growth, force/friction or fluid solver.

**Observed scope:** C1 leaves penetrated pavement; final C5 40 candidates/30 emitted have bounded continuous footprint and local blocker checks. Factory 111 sampled foot poses are nonpenetrating, 109 accepted.

- C1 roots were supported while seven non-root vertices per plant entered pavement, up to about 143.96 mm. Test the leaf/prototype envelope; final selected C5 emits no weeds rather than pretending that rooting alone fixes the collision.
- Final C5 checks all 40 actual bottom footprints, 30 emitted. Deepest embedding is 0.646209 mm within the declared 0.8 mm allowance. Full prototype triangle intersections and two-way containment find zero collisions against 34 exported local non-ground blockers.

- Preserve intended anchoring/embedding tolerances explicitly. A local clearance proof is not global structural stability or continuous interpolation between the 37 rigid history poses.
