## Explicit geometry brief

“The chiseled geometry in the last image is perfect. No rounded smooth curves.
Just edges and flat surfaces that approximate a curve.”

“Ok just be sure the flat surfaces aren't uniform quads or triangles. Thats easy.”

These are project-specific design constraints. Approximate curvature with
purposeful, uneven, form-following planes of varied sizes, angles and spacing,
including staggered cuts. Do not substitute uniform radial segment rings with
flat shading, random triangulation or cubic mannequins. Individual quads and
triangles are permitted; uniform repetitive tessellation is the rejected design.
No actual geometry realization is certified by this release.
