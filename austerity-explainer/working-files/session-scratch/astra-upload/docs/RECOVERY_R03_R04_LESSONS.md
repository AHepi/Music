## 1. Choose reference-scale atmosphere and setting before micro-tuning

Prioritize the largest reference-facing color/value and setting gap; a local technical repair or tiny shader change does not settle the painted brief.

### Recorded evidence

R03 retained stone/wood and contact gains but left large atmosphere/setting gaps. Final R04 art review records useful mine sky/settlement, street roof/sky and factory luminous-air gains while eight bounded art targets remain. This is attributed comparison evidence, not a new art verdict.

### Reusable practice
- Compare large masses at thumbnail scale first, then inspect full-size edges and a useful close/changed-view proof. More fine noise cannot repair a weak large-shape design.
- Use actual modeled ridge/cottage/terrace depth for setting gaps; use spatial sky/air and native light where the reference calls for atmosphere.
- Build one prominent figure exemplar in neutral front/three-quarter/side views, then test production-light hero reinsertion before propagating it. R04 deliberately held figures; no completed redesign is claimed.
- Keep a finite prioritized cycle and retain a prior winner when a test loses. The user’s explicit all-clear controls an ongoing authorized art iteration; a deadline is not artistic completion.

### Scope and coordinates

One declared scene stream and an isolated exemplar before propagation; Original reference viewport, actual same-camera hero and clearly labeled thumbnail analysis; ignore text/player/portrait overlays; Preserve known gains, freeze branch baselines and show a proposed camera change separately from the original-camera comparison.

## 2. Test sky hemisphere, seam and lighting branches explicitly

A generated panorama becomes a useful environment only after orientation, seam and actual scene-response checks; a World sky is at infinity.

### Recorded evidence

R04 street retains an unsuccessful hemisphere stage, a corrected direction/azimuth/camera-strength branch and a rejected -14 degree latitude test. Its final generated image is 1774x887 with measured nonzero horizontal-edge differences. Mine metadata records upper-half seam mean absolute difference 6.5538/255. These are diagnostics, not mathematical seamlessness or HDR calibration.

### Reusable practice
- Inspect a sky-only asset for forbidden scene objects, baked foreground and overlays before use. A generated concept remains supplemental; an actual render establishes implementation.
- Inspect orientation before tuning many rotations. Keep the unsuccessful/rejected sky tests labeled. Street hemisphere correction also changed camera-branch strength, so do not call that comparison rotation-only.
- A low average seam difference can hide a local discontinuity. Inspect the seam and poles visually; rotate the seam away from the primary view as a mitigation, never as a proof of seamlessness.
- Preserving a diffuse ambient branch while changing camera/glossy appearance is an intentional control, not evidence that every ray sees identical lighting. Check reflections and changed-camera coherence.
- Use real local plume volumes and actual setting meshes when parallax or source contact matters. An environment at infinity cannot establish local cloud or cottage depth.

### Scope and coordinates

Packed direction-sampled World environment, with camera/glossy/diffuse branches identified separately; 2:1 equirectangular sphere directions; finite local smoke remains separate scene geometry; Check hemisphere, longitude seam, poles and latitude with an actual hero plus small camera turn; declare any strength/ambient change alongside orientation.

Not established:
- No new runtime/GUI installation qualification, scene rebuild or independent image inspection in 0.1.6.
- No user artistic all-clear, universal deformation/remeshing or all-space contact certificate.
- Claiming nonzero edge metrics prove mathematical seamlessness
- Treating a generated LDR painting as a calibrated HDR radiance capture
- Calling a small camera-turn check a full-sphere/seam/pole certification

## 3. Build real setting forms and validate component winding

Real geometry and outward closed components are necessary construction checks; specific landform/building design still needs image judgment.

### Recorded evidence

R04 street independently checks 61 new evaluated owners (56 nonempty sources plus five GN outputs), 80 local opening witnesses and five roof/chimney overlaps. Five inward gable prisms were repaired in v02 and retained in v03. Mine has 98 new evaluated setting owners, but cottages remain solid wall bodies with attached window/recess/glazing details, not cut-through apertures.

### Reusable practice
- Inspect finite positions, boundary/nonmanifold/wire edges, orientation conflicts and signed volume per closed component. A closed inward shell can pass nonmanifold counts; positive total volume can conceal one inverted component.
- Preserve the five gable loop corrections rather than equating their negligible current hero difference with an unnecessary repair. Do not infer enclosed volume from an open or split-face mesh signed sum.
- Show real depth in an alternate view. Street local aperture witnesses do not make every opening externally visible: 22 scene rays were occluded by neighboring terraces.
- Keep real ridge/cottage volumes, then design slope-following planes and coherent multi-bay recession. Giant triangular sheets, plain cottages and an end-wall street remain unresolved art limitations.
- A post-construction neutral-material diagnostic is not a historical pre-detail massing stage. Preserve exact stage labels.

### Scope and coordinates

Explicit new or changed ridge, terrace, cottage, roof and evaluated procedural owners; Mesh/component geometry with actual openings, inset witnesses and local contact coordinates; Check each disconnected component and evaluated instance before selection; recheck affected opening/roof contacts after edits.

## 4. Check terrain contact across the whole affected footprint

Terrain changes require topmost evaluated ground and intersection checks across foundation centers, corners and edge midpoints, including inherited owners in the changed footprint.

### Recorded evidence

Three first-delivered mine foundations were buried by about 0.258, 1.621 and 4.374 m above their tops. The +9.5 m world-Y near-ridge repair changes only one transform. Its E checkpoint records 36 successful grade samples and zero tested cottage/ridge surface intersections; reopened final v02 separately checks four centers, approximately 0.03 m body/foundation overlap and four roof/chimney common interiors.

### Reusable practice
- Do not stop at a center ray or an AABB intersection. Sample the four corners and four edge midpoints as well as the center for each changed foundation; inspect evaluated terrain, not only source vertex minima.
- The 36-sample E checkpoint and final four-center/contact proof are complementary recorded evidence. Do not relabel final center-only checks as a new full-footprint sweep.
- A narrow isolated contact image can clip a foundation edge. Label hidden unrelated owners and framing limitations; keep a full-scene off-axis view alongside the diagnostic.
- Preserve first-delivered and repaired files. The siting repair is complete in selected mine v02, while terrain paint and cottage design remain open.

### Scope and coordinates

New setting plus all existing support/structure owners intersecting the changed spatial footprint; Evaluated world-space topmost grade and foundation vertical spans; actual roof/chimney common interiors; Repeat the focused perimeter/grade and intersection tests after terrain translation or GN relief changes, then inspect an actual contact view before freezing.

- No user artistic all-clear, universal deformation/remeshing or all-space contact certificate.
- A scene-wide collision or future-terrain certificate

## 6. Design wear between camouflage and clean-polygon extremes

Use broad unequal worn color groups, purposeful middle-scale directional boundaries and sparse fine relief, with quiet interiors and native material response.

### Recorded evidence

R04 factory retains the R03 floor. Independently inspected rejected C2 increases blue/ochre scale but repeats rounded coastline-like edges; rejected D has overly clean large polygon bands. Both change pigment and inherited pigment-derived roughness, not albedo alone. Street paving remains held with equal amber rims and separate pools. Neither unresolved target is reported fixed.

### Reusable practice
- Retain R03’s repaired Mesh_00139 coverage across all 139 active floor owners. Fine mottling and long bands are an art-design issue, not a missing-UV diagnosis.
- Start locally with a small palette, unequal directional wear groups and selectively chipped/scumbled edges. Avoid both round camouflage and giant pristine polygons; a new random seed does not change the vocabulary.
- Keep pigment distinct from relief. Do not raise every pigment island into equally prominent crust; disclose any roughness coupling when attributing a test.
- Judge color/value groups at hero scale and real response in off-axis/side-light views before propagation. A generated flat texture is an input candidate, not an implemented success.
- For street paving, preserve dry crisp islands and genuine sky reflections. Test a small crown/bevel change only if material grouping leaves the equal rim pattern dominant. Reject all-over gloss, cyan painted stripes and image blur.

### Scope and coordinates

Bounded visible floor/cobble owners and their face-used effective material routes; Stable floor-assembly/mesh coordinates with meaningful physical mark scale and verified owner coverage; Hold selected R04 B2 lighting/air for floor tests; compare response-only and any geometry-profile branch separately.

## 8. Qualify metric mapping by actual face basis and consumer

Finite stored coordinates and equal axis scale do not alone establish isotropic physical scale on oblique faces or active consumption by every owner.

### Recorded evidence

R03’s 925 stone-owner UVs are finite equal-axis dominant-plane charts, approximately 1.05 m/UV on principal faces. Recorded edge samples span 1.049992–1.758654 m/UV and a representative oblique bevel has about 1.729 anisotropy, without an established visible defect. R04’s strict tangent-basis claim covers only ten new source limestone/threshold owners, not inherited charts or scaled GN prototypes.

### Reusable practice
- Keep the precise dominant-plane qualification instead of promising metric isotropy. Use an orthonormal tangent basis or designed continuous chart when a visible need justifies remapping.
- Do not remap 925 owners solely to manufacture a technical defect. Recorded bevel distortion has not been established as visibly harmful in the selected imagery.
- The 139-owner factory common assembly mapping and small UV error are coverage evidence. Mesh_00139 retains negative-V values with EXTEND outside the current witnessed strip; check new framing rather than inventing a current missing-UV failure.
- Preserve the earlier seven-stored/three-active-consumer mine qualification. Stored fields, active shader consumption, rigid movement and arbitrary deformation are different claims.

### Scope and coordinates

Actual face-used material consumers and the specific inspected surface charts; World metres versus saved per-face tangent basis or explicitly qualified dominant-plane projection; Test basis/scale, active consumption and relevant views; recheck atlas bounds when camera/framing changes expose new regions.
