===== pages-and-timing
--- summary
I built the page timing tools. All three run, and their tests pass: 80 checks in my 3 test files. The whole calculators suite also passes (17 of 17 files). I changed nothing in the film kit, spent nothing and did not commit.

What I built:
1. page_events.mjs runs a cinema chapter in the stage without filming it. Every 30th of a second it writes down what the pages do:
   - every word lookup, with every time that word is said on the page;
   - each shot, slide and join;
   - every flat graphic (words, labels, cards, rings, tags, arrows), with its box in pixels and how visible it is;
   - the held-shot value and the live effects.
   To make this possible I added notes to the plugin's graphics.js and cinema-kit.js. The notes only work when the tool switches them on, and filming never does. Each fade-in number now has a name, and graphics.js exports the table as APPEAR_DELAYS (same values).
   - Proof that normal drawing is unchanged: 5 stills of the test chapter came out byte-for-byte the same three ways: with the notes on, with them off, and with the film kit's original files.
   - Control: changing one fade by 0.01 s changes 33,822 pixels by up to 13 levels, so the comparison can see a change.
   - Running time: pages 3 to 5 of chapter 2 take about 2.5 minutes and needed about 2 GB at peak; old chapter 2 takes 12 seconds.
2. plan_page_timing.py works out the timing from those events:
   - lookups that don't say which time a repeated word is meant;
   - the pause after each page, following the skeptic's rule;
   - reading times, using each kind's appear delay read from graphics.js;
   - conclusions, words fading a frame before a cut, and a new line drawn over a fading one;
   - margins, typed words against the narration, numbers against a ledger, and counts that land on their word and hold;
   - word groups per page, slides tied to words, and stretches where nothing changes;
   - the list of stills to check (as one render command) and the planned cuts for find_jumps.py.
   It can also read lookups straight from page files (no browser), and list which filmed chunks to redo after a re-record.
3. check_page_layout.mjs is the slim pose sampler. It finds:
   - pops and one-frame jumps outside planned cuts;
   - effects that depend on what was drawn before (lesson 29), or are shared between pages;
   - held-shot keys while the camera moves, and holds with no held-shot key;
   - things drawn in the same place at each event.
4. A small made-up test chapter (page-events-test.js). It has a clean version and one with 15 planted faults copied from the project's own findings.

What it found:
- Chapter 2 for newcomers, pages 3 to 5:
  - 8 lookups (workers, machines, In, More, spare, More, prices, up) don't say which time they mean. Each happens to take the right one.
  - MACHINES is readable for only 0.84 s. Starting it on "and" would give 1.05 s.
  - The timeline's pause after page 5 is 3.5 s; the join needs 3.0 s.
  - Round 4 said every line ends by 0.86 across. In fact WHAT HAPPENS TO PRICES? reaches 0.897 (0.895 measured on the film), still inside the 0.90 limit.
  - The layout check finds nothing to fix, which agrees with round 4's film.
- Old chapter 2 (shipped in log 20): 83 things to fix under the newer rules, most of them reading times under 1 s and ambiguous lookups. I confirmed one by eye on a rendered frame: on page 8 the PRICES UP label covers the F of FLAT OUT.
  - Not a fault: the tool once said BARELY MOVE jumped 585 px in one frame. The render showed nothing, because the words hadn't appeared yet, so I changed the check to judge only what shows.

--- api
Events:
- Command: node paper-film/tools/page_events.mjs SCENE OUT.json
- Options:
  - --pages 3,4,5
  - --from S / --to S
  - --step N
  - --order-check N (default 15; 0 turns it off)
  - --query a=b
  - --kit DIR
  - --serve /URL=DIR (repeatable; for assets or node_modules kept elsewhere)
  - --render-stills DIR T1 T2 ... (draws stills with the notes switched on)
  - --help
- Works on cinema chapters only. Run from the plugin with a symlinked kit, it needs node --preserve-symlinks --preserve-symlinks-main. PAPER_FILM_PLAYWRIGHT can point at playwright-core.
- Output JSON:
  - fps, frame_zero_seconds, frame_size, appear_delays, text_left, text_right
  - pages: number, words, first and last word, join, camera_plans
  - lookups: page, which, word, n, start, said[], asked_from
  - frames: f, t, and per page: shot, page_still, camera [x,y,z,qx,qy,qz,qw,fov], effects_shown, effects_key; plus join
  - items: id, kind, fn, text, at, until, fadeOut, fullAt, group or within, runs, and track [[frame, opacity, box, words_opacity, drawn, counter_text]]
  - effects (runs per live thing), live_things, shared_live_things, order_differences

Notes in the page code:
- graphics.js calls globalThis.pageEvents.note({kind, fn, text, at, until, fadeOut, fullAt, opacity, box, ...}) only when globalThis.pageEvents?.on === true.
- graphics.js exports APPEAR_DELAYS (buildWords 0.14, callout 0.55, calloutWordsWithLine 0.12, hook, counter, credit, placeTitle, ruleCard, guessRing, nameTag, priceArrowWord, dotPanel, lineChart, bars).
- cinema-kit.js wordClock calls globalThis.pageEvents.lookup({...}) only when the notes are on.

===== script-sound-budget
--- summary
I built three tools and tested them on the project's own files: a script checker for before and after recording, sound checks wired into the mix and finishing scripts, and a planner for money and filming. All three test files pass (43, 33 and 47 checks). I spent nothing and did not commit. I left the film kit, the round 5 Works files and the checkout files alone.

1. narration-tools/lint_script.py (the script checker)
- **Before recording**, these stop the paid step:
  - the cue at the start of each page, which must be the one accent line on every page;
  - words in capitals;
  - names not in their sound spelling;
  - sentences over 20 words, or more than 12 on average;
  - more than 6 spoken figures a minute of speech (the recorded chapter 2 has 8.1; the newcomer script has 3.6);
  - the main rule not said twice word for word;
  - no page marked "key": true, or a key page faster than the median page;
  - facts in the research ledger whose valid_until date has passed.
- **It works out:**
  - the 4 s guess holds, found from the text (pages 4, 8 and 10 of the newcomer script);
  - a 2 s card hold for pages marked "key" or "held_card";
  - the estimated timeline, by calling estimate_timeline.py unchanged;
  - words a minute per page;
  - credits and dollars.
- **Lists for a person:** unfamiliar words, names, the first 30 seconds (are the stakes there?), repeated words with every sentence they appear in, years said out of order, and who is quoted.
- **After recording** it finds: a cue read aloud, transcript words that differ from the script, names heard wrongly, how much real silence each "..." left, words placed late after a pause, and missing downloads. It ends with the pages to record again and their cost.

2. narration-tools/sound_gates.py (the sound checks)
- length, loudness, alignment, the music under the voice (gaps and duck), voice steadiness, mouth levels, and where to start a soundtrack (music-start).
- Wired in as gates that stop with a FAIL line:
  - mix_narration_over_music.sh: lengths, plus (with a timeline) the music 18 dB under her voice and alignment with the narration;
  - finish_film.sh: lengths, -15 LUFS and true peak on the encoded copy, size, and alignment. It now takes the timeline as a 6th argument; without it, it ends with exit 3;
  - add_music_bed.sh and mix_voice_over_music.sh.

3. calculators/plan_budget.py (the money and filming planner)
- **Money:** prices paid steps counting the pictures sent as well as the one returned, and flags a sent picture with no size. Keeps the spend ledger counted from the allowance's start date and refuses a step bigger than what is left.
- **Filming:** reads each frame's kind from the page events, plans from a short profile filmed first (the table is only a fallback), and gives workers, piece size, lazy loading (from the measured memory per set), memory, disk, and stretches under 2 hours with their FILM_FROM/FILM_TO commands.
- **Also:** a check before pushing files, and a status command for a filming run.

**Findings:**
- **The mix comes out short on this machine.** The film kit's mix script now makes chapter 2's mix 0.36 s short, because ffmpeg 6.1's sidechaincompress stops early. The new length gate caught it. I fixed the plugin copy by padding the ducked music; its mix is otherwise identical to the old one, sample for sample.
- **The notice corner's music clip from log 32 has a true peak of -0.9 dBTP**, just over the -1 limit. add_music_bed.sh now aims at -1.5, and its output measures -1.5.
- **The ducking measure was chosen to fit the record.** Measured page by page, it gives chapter 1 at -14: 21.3 dB and at -11: 18.3 dB (11.8 in pauses), against the recorded 21, 18 and 12. A word-by-word measure reads about 2 dB higher.
- **The per-word voice rule would send back every page of the accepted chapter 2.** Those pages have 4 to 12 "raised" words each. Now only a page's typical pitch sends it back; raised and loud words are a list for a person.
- **The cue rule judged the whole cue, which gave false alarms.** A whole cue stretches to 0.51 s when the first word is placed late, so it now judges each cue word.
- **Filming time:** fitted rates predict each chapter 2 filming of pages 3 to 5 within 1% when each chunk is predicted from the other chunks. A profile of 8 stretches gets within about 5%. The round 4 table misses round 3 by 25% and round 5 by 52%.

--- api
Every command takes --json, --out FILE, --report FILE and --settings FILE, and ends with the film-settings.json values it used.

**narration-tools/lint_script.py**
- `before SCRIPT.json [--word-times EARLIER.json | --timeline T.json] [--timeline-out OUT.json] [--default-pause S] [--ledger L.json --today DATE] [--common-words FILE]` exits 1 on errors.
- `after SCRIPT.json --transcripts DIR [--recordings DIR] [--heard DIR]` exits 1 if a page must be recorded again.
- Script fields it reads: accent_instruction, phonetic_names, main_rule (a string or a list), allowed_capitals. Per page: key, held_card, guess, pause_after, ledger (a list of ids).
- Ledger: {claims: [{id, claim, said_by, valid_until}]}.
- Python: lint_before(...), lint_after(...), spoken_figures(text), sentences_of(text), pauses_after(script, settings, default_pause).

**narration-tools/sound_gates.py** (each command exits 1 on a FAIL)
- `length FILE --expected S [--step NAME] [--stream audio]`
- `loudness FILE [--music-only] [--target L] [--limiter-limit X]` (prints the limiter value to use when the peak is over)
- `alignment FILM MIX_OR_NARRATION TIMELINE`
- `gaps VOICE_STEM MUSIC_STEM TIMELINE`
- `duck MUSIC NARRATION TIMELINE [--music-level DB] [--louder DB]` (prints MUSIC_LEVEL_DB)
- `voice WORD_TIMES [--recordings-from DIR]`
- `mouth NARRATION [--timeline T]`
- `music-start MUSIC --seconds N [--mood quiet|lively]`
- Python: music_gaps(...), render_stems(music, narration, level, folder), loudness_of(path), voice_gate(...).

**Shell changes**
- mix_narration_over_music.sh MUSIC NARRATION OUT.wav OUT.mp3 [TIMELINE]. Environment: MUSIC_LEVEL_DB, STEMS_DIR, STEMS_ONLY=1.
- finish_film.sh SILENT MIX MASTER SEND [SIZE_MB] [TIMELINE]. Exit 1 on a failed gate; exit 3 if no timeline was given.
- add_music_bed.sh and mix_voice_over_music.sh: same arguments as before, now with gates.
- find_loud_words.py: adds word_peak_levels(audio, words); its printout is unchanged, checked byte for byte.

**calculators/plan_budget.py**
- `cost PLAN.json [--rate C --rate-date D] [--ledger L --allowance A]` exits 2 if the plan is over what is left.
- `ledger L.json [--allowance A] [--today D] [--next-cost $]` exits 2 to refuse.
- `frames EVENTS[.gz]`
- `profile-commands EVENTS --scene S`
- `measure LOGS... --events EVENTS [--leave-out-chunks]` (writes rates)
- `filming EVENTS --scene S [--rates R.json] [--from-frame A --to-frame B] [--processors N] [--free-memory-gb G] [--heavy-pages N] [--views-per-page N] [--blender-running yes|no|auto] [--free-disk-gb G]` exits 1 when the plan does not fit.
- `disk --seconds S`
- `push [PATHS] [--git REPO]` exits 2 on a refused file.

- Python: classify_frames, frame_mix, chunk_list, read_logs, fit_rates, measure_rates, predict_seconds, plan_filming, cost_plan, ledger_state, check_push.
