# 12 Plan - chapter 1 in paper pop-up

**What this is:** the plan for making all ten pages of chapter 1 in the paper pop-up look you
approved ("This is great!", log 12). Nothing in it has been painted yet.
Source for the story: Clara E. Mattei, *The Capital Order* (2022). Facts: research entries C1-01 to C1-29.

## Rules every page follows

- Painted still pictures moved as paper: pieces flip up from flat, slide in, drop in, fold away.
  No hands, no cartoon movement.
- One painted background per page (the mine in page 3 is its second page). Pages change by peeling
  from the bottom-right corner; pieces fold down first.
- A figure that "speaks" has a paper jaw that moves with Malina's voice.
- Words on screen are few, plain and only there to help memory; never dialogue or subtitles.
- No real person is drawn. Mattei appears only as her name and her book.
- Every picture is checked for history mistakes, symbols and flags before it goes in.

## What needs painting

About 70 pictures: 10 backgrounds and about 60 figures and objects. Backgrounds and big figures at
full size (about 10 US cents each); small objects at a quarter size (about 3 US cents each).
Estimate: about US$5, and up to US$8 with re-paints. Cut-outs cost under 1 cent each.
Page 3's tank will be re-painted to match the crowds' painting style (about 10 cents).

## Making it

Each page is its own scene file, and all ten play as one continuous film on the chapter's
narration and mix. Filming takes about 1 second a frame: about 2 hours of computer time for the
whole chapter (3 minutes 41 seconds), at no cost.

## Traps

- Video and image models add things nobody asked for: check every picture before use.
- Keep the painting style the same across pages: the same style words in every request.
- Page 6 carries the most words (five cards); if it feels busy, the cards can become pictures.
- The tank is a symbol of power, not a claim that tanks faced the crowd (research C1-10).
