===== The tool does most of its job. On the eight measured scenes its turn and tilt signs agree with Blender to within 0.4 px for every light and for where the lines meet. The camera-height, rule 1, rule 12 slide/turn and reference-size formulas check out (metres per pixel = distance/focal). Re-run with the current tool, all seven planted faults still come out as problem 1, except d, where the pail appears only at the end of problem 1's fix. But it gives wrong answers in cases close to the ones it was built for. A prop sunk into the pavement is called floating, and the fix says to lower it further. A prop just past the frame edge is called "hidden behind the set". The camera's lean (roll) has the opposite sign in the projection. A check file with no "lines" always fails rule 1. A landscape frame with the portrait reference gets "turn 16.3 degrees" as Fix first. Several checks can never fail: a prop up to 40% cut off at the bottom or left, a title zone given in pixels or with no area. Three unusual inputs crash it with a raw traceback.
