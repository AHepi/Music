## What it built
A British army camp around 1915 at dusk after rain (no period in the request, so the look page's). A muddy camp road to a vanishing point in the upper middle; six long timber huts with rust-streaked corrugated roofs, boarded walls, stove pipes and a few small lit windows; the hero a timber watchtower with a lit cabin, dark against a low amber glow and hills, a faint searchlight beam (a thin cone of lit haze) crossing the upper-left sky. Middle and foreground: a field gun turned across the road, a duckboard path, crates with brass shells standing in front, a hurricane lantern, sandbags, a coil of barbed wire along the bottom edge, mud lumps and stones, fence posts and wire, lamp posts, bell tents far off. Water only where the ground dips, so the puddles are long and broken and mirror the sky, lantern and windows. No people, hands or words; the flag plain.

## Light and framing
Eye 1.65 m, 36 mm lens, vanishing point near 40 percent from the top. One very low amber sun behind the tower with a matching sky glow; about nine lit windows, four lamp posts, the lantern and the cabin, each with a real light outside its glow; everything else teal and slate; thin blue haze thicker low and far. Finishing in the script: glow round bright spots, vignette, darks pushed to blue-black, dim warm tones pulled to slate, a light soften.

## What it fixed
Sky orange everywhere (wrong coordinates); too dark behind tall hills; too bright and milky; puddles one flood; tower too small; empty bottom and foil-like ground; white lamp balls; warm share out of band (a slate grade brought it back); white windows and pale ground; lying shells like a yellow plank (stood upright); drums and black cubes removed; the gun a dark box until turned a quarter across the road; the wire coil as black ticks until the ring angles were fixed; stronger window and lamp lights for warm pools.

## Traps it checked against
Lights outside the glass; thin haze; broken puddles; dark ground; no smoke; foreground props kept within the narrow frame; a thin beam rather than a wall of lit glass; few lit windows; no words or people.

## What it did not do
No smoke; rust only faint (the iron is dark in this light); huts simple boxes without eaves, drainpipes or peeling paint; tents mostly hidden; crates and sandbags boxy; some CG-clean parts (wheel rims, repeated stones); the finish only slightly brushy; no 64-sample full size.
