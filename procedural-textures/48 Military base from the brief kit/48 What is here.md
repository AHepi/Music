# 48 Military base from the brief kit

What this folder is: the military base built a third time, by a fresh Sonnet builder given only the brief kit (`place-brief/`: the look page, seven reference pictures, the traps page, the measure tool), 80 minutes on the whole machine (log 48; decision 62, steps 1 and 2 of the plan).

## What happened

It started at 18:03 and stopped at 18:36 by its own choice, with 49 minutes left, after 24 pictures (the pictures-only builder drew 20 in 51 minutes; the plugin builder 2 in 12). It measured every picture with the kit's tool, read the traps page and checked its picture against it, and avoided every trap on the list (its lamps lit, its haze thin, its puddles broken, its foreground inside the narrow frame). The brief's "keep fixing until the clock" did not keep it going to the end.

## Traps
