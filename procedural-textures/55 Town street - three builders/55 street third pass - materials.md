# Materials: the town street, third pass (the fronts)

What this file is: the third builder's list, written before the work and kept true during it. The first builder's list (`second pass/materials - first pass.md`) and the second builder's list (`second pass/materials - second pass.md`) still hold for everything already in the place. This list is only what the third pass adds or changes: the buildings' faces and the middle distance, above the bottom third of the frame. Layout, camera, lights and the near ground stay as they were.

## Who uses these fronts (they are not in the picture)

- **Who is here:** the families in the flats up every stair; the grocer (right, R3) and the dairyman (left, L4) who keep the shops; the factor (the landlord's man) who has not mended a broken window in a year.
- **What they do all day:** burn coal in every grate, so soot falls on every ledge and the rain carries it down the stone; open and shut their sashes, pull their blinds and curtains; the grocer and the dairyman hang out their signs and stand goods at their doors; nobody paints anything.
- **What they left when they stepped out of frame:** blinds pulled down to different heights and net curtains half drawn in the dark windows; a box of geraniums on the sill of the flat over the end shop; the stair window the factor boarded over; the grocer's hanging sign (a teapot, no words) over his shop window and his sack barrow against his stall riser; a costermonger's barrow backed against R4 beside the stair door its owner lives up; the dairyman's blank sign board on its bracket; the close's blank name board over the passage.

### Wear on every front you can see (R2, R3, R4 and its gable on the wynd; L4, L5; E1, E2, E3)

- **soot runs under the sills** · coal soot washed off each sill by the rain · black-brown, strongest just under the sill, running down 1 to 1.6 m and breaking into streaks · under every upper sill; longer and stronger on the far fronts so they show at 35 m · (made in code: a strong see-through soot of my own on planes laid by the store's `stain.on_wall`)
- **soot over the window heads** · soot from the room's fire and the street · a dark smudge on the stone over the lintel, fading upwards · over about half the windows, the upper floors most · (made in code, same soot)
- **soot under the eaves** · soot where the overhang keeps the rain off · near-black, a band 1.5 to 2.5 m deep with a ragged lower edge · under every cornice and gutter · (made in code, same soot)
- **soot on the end chimney stacks** · coal smoke rolling over the caps · black for 1.7 m down from each cap, fading below · the two stacks of the end tenement (E2) against the sky · (made in code, the far soot)
- **rising damp** · ground water climbing the stone · green-black at the foot, a ragged tide line 0.8 to 1.3 m up, a paler salt edge on its top · along the foot of every front, worst beside the doors and under the downpipes · (made in code: a damp of my own on `stain.on_wall` planes)
- **gutter leaks** · rain overflowing a sagging cast-iron gutter at its joints · dark green-black streaks 1.5 to 3 m long down the wall from the eaves, moss at their tops · two or three per front, under the gutter's joints · (made in code, the damp)
- **downpipe stains** · water leaking at each socket of the downpipe · a dark wet stripe down the stone behind the pipe, worst under each socket, a dark splash where the shoe spills on the flags · behind every downpipe you can see · (made in code, the damp; the splash with the store's `stain.on_ground`, damp, thin and off the walking line)
- **rust runs** · rust washed out of the iron · orange-brown runs fading downwards · under each downpipe holdfast, under the hopper heads, under the sign brackets · (store `MADE • stain rust` on `stain.on_wall` planes)
- **paint peeling** · the doors', sashes' and shop fronts' dark paint · flaked off in big patches to grey timber, worst on the bottom rails · every painted timber thing on the fronts · (store `WOOD • peeling dark painted doors`, with flaking patches made in code, big enough to survive the side view)

## Tried and taken out

- Pots of geraniums and a milk jug on the sills of R2 and R3: the near lamp's head hides the first-floor sills in the frame, and the upper floors are too dark for anything on a sill to show (the jug drew as one pale dot). A thing nobody can name is noise, so they came out.

## Traps

- The stone tool's block faces stand out to about 0.23 m; a stain plane nearer the wall than that is cut by the blocks and hardly shows (the first pass's stains stood at 0.21 m). The third pass's stains stand at 0.236 m on ashlar, 0.085 m on rubble.

- Stains on a front 15 to 35 m away do not show unless they are big and strong; every stain is judged in a drawn picture at full size, never by its numbers.
- Things laid flat on the side fronts are seen edge-on from the camera (the street runs away from it); things that stand out from a wall (signs, brackets, pots on sills) read there; flat things read on the end fronts, which face the camera.
- Nothing new hides the hero shop, stands in a lit doorway or goes on the near ground the second builder dressed; the brightest place stays the end shop.
- Signs and boards stay blank: no words.
- Keep this list true during the work: a thing that changes in the scene changes here.
