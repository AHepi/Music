### What I built
A tenement street in a northern British town in about 1915, at dusk after rain. You stand by the right-hand kerb and look straight down the street. A four-storey tenement closes the far end. Its lit shop window and the gas lamp in front of it are the warm centre of the picture.

- **Street:** setts, each a slightly different shade, with kerbstones, flagstone pavements and water along both gutters. Seven puddles, each with a ragged edge, a dark wet rim and a ripple. The big near one mirrors two lamps and a lit window, and the middle one mirrors the shop. Near your feet there is a cast-iron drain grate, a round sewer cover and weeds in the joints.
- **Buildings:** 13 tenements of three to five storeys.
  - Fronts are stone blocks in relief; one front is rough rubble stone.
  - Windows have sills, lintels and frames; doors have stone steps.
  - Slate roofs overhang the walls, with an eaves board, gutter and rafter ends underneath.
  - There are downpipes, chimney stacks with clay pots, and stone gables.
  - Two dormers sit on the far roof, one with a lit room.
  - Soot, damp and rust stains are on the fronts.
- **Shops:** three, all with blank sign boards and no words. The dairy and the grocer are dimly lit. The end shop is lit, and you can see shelves of jars and tins and a counter through its window.
- **A close:** a passage cut through the end tenement next to the shop, with a lamp inside and cooler light from the back court beyond.
- **Lamps and lit windows:** five lit gas lamps. The lamp nearest you is not lit yet, as if the lamplighter hasn't got to it. A few windows are lit, with curtains half drawn.
- **Things people left:**
  - The coal merchant's cart, its shafts resting on props.
  - The grocer's barrel and crates.
  - At the stair door nearest you, glowing from inside: a broom and a bucket beside the step, and the coal scuttle on the step.
  - Further off, the dairy's milk churns and an umbrella.
- **Sky:** smoke drifts from the end chimney.

### Light and framing
- **Light:** the one warm centre is the end shop and its lamp. A cool moonlight from behind-left lights the right-hand fronts and the road. Over that is the dusk sky and thin haze. Small warm lights sit by every lit window, door and the close. One unseen lamp off the left edge lights the setts near you.
- **Frame:** 24 mm lens, eye height 1.62 m, about 0.85 m from the right kerb, looking straight down the street.
  - The vanishing point is just above the middle, at the shop.
  - The kerb line runs in from the bottom right, and the unlit lamp frames the right side.
  - The bottom third holds setts, the puddle, the sewer cover, the grate and weeds.

### Checked and fixed

- **Plain shapes:** the plain tall wall seen above a lower roof now has rubble stone on it; the chimney smoke reads as faint smoke; the cart is moved into view and readable; the close is lit.
- **Checklist:** every surface in the material list is laid, all puddles have a ragged edge, wet rim and ripple, and every building has eaves, an overhang and stones in relief. No words and no people. The material list was kept true.

### New traps the next builder would hit
- The stain tool sets its stains 4 mm from the wall, which puts them behind stone fronts built with the store's stone tools (the blocks stand 20 cm out). Set them further out.
- Thin gutter water next to a lamp post mirrors the post as a black cut-out.
- Small puddles near you mirror the dark fronts, not the sky, and read as black blots.
- A lit lamp near you steals the eye from the hero.
- A light placed in front of a shop window shows as a white slab unless it is hidden from the camera.
- Things at a dark wall foot can't be named until a light of their own reaches them.
- A taller building's side above a lower neighbour shows as a plain wall.
