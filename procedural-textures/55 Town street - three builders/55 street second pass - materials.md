# Materials: the town street, second pass (dressing)

What this file is: the dresser's list, written before dressing and kept true during it. The first builder's list (`first pass/materials.md`) still holds for everything already in the place; this list is only what the second pass adds or changes. Layout, camera and lights stay as the first builder left them.

## Who uses this place (they are not in the picture)

- **Who is here:** the families up the stair of the near right-hand tenement; the coal merchant and the dairyman, whose horses stand at the kerbs; the corporation's road men, who lifted and relaid the setts round the sewer cover near the eye last week.
- **What they do all day:** carry ash down the stair and coal up it; cart coal and milk over the setts and leave their horses standing; the road men dig, relay and ram setts and move on.
- **What they left when they stepped out of frame:** the stair's ash bins carried down and stood at the kerb by the near lamp post for the morning's cart, one lid off and leant against its bin, cinders spilt in the gutter; the road men's spare setts stacked at the kerb across from their patch, waiting for the corporation's cart, with their lantern left burning on top to mark them; straw from the horses' feed, mud off the wheels and the town's leaves washed into the gutter and caught at the drain grate.

### The open road near the eye (surface only: nothing stands in the road)

- **relaid patch round the sewer cover** · new granite setts, paler and less worn than the rest, joints run with black pitch · a week old: sharp arrises, pitch still glossy, setts not yet sunk to the old ones' level · pitch shining in every joint; grit swept into the edges where it meets the old setts · (store `COBBLES • wet basalt` copy, lighter, sharper; pitch made in code)
- **horse stains** · dung and urine soaked into the joints where horses stood while the coal went up the stair · dark brown, soaked in, not lying on top · two thin patches near the eye, off the puddles · (store `stain` tool, mud, thin)
- **the old setts, from two quarries** · granite and whinstone setts · each its own shade already (first builder); now about a third stained warm brown (iron in the stone, rust off the iron tyres) and about one in eight blue-black whinstone · over the whole road, so the near ground is not one grey · (made in code on the first builder's `COBBLES • wet basalt`: a second shade picked per sett)
- **rocked setts** · the old setts near the eye · about one in six rocked loose by the wheels: sunk a centimetre or so and tipped, so each catches the sky its own way · the near road outside the patch · (first builder's setts moved in code)
- **missing setts** · the old road · three setts gone, the holes holding water over grit · two by the gutter near the eye where wheels turn in to the kerb, one in the left wheel line · (old setts lifted in code; store `puddle` tool in each hole)
- **grit, small stones and moss in the joints** · grit and road dirt packed into the joints; moss where wheels do not reach · dark, wet · every joint of the old setts near the eye; moss only in the last half metre before the kerb · (made in code: a grit bed just under the setts' tops, and small lumps that show only in the joints)

### The gutter and kerb near the eye (right)

- **gutter water** · rain water running down the channel to the grate · a thin ragged run · along the right kerb from the frame's bottom to the grate · (store `puddle` tool)
- **gutter silt** · grit, mud and coal dust washed down the channel · a dark soft band at the foot of the kerb, thickest just above the drain grate · along the right kerb from the frame's bottom to the near lamp · (store `stain` tool, mud and coal dust, thin)
- **straw, leaves and a sodden paper in the gutter** · horses' straw (trodden dark), fallen leaves blown down the street, one sheet of newspaper gone to pulp (no print showing) · wet, flattened, dark · caught against the drain grate and in the angle of the kerb, where the water left them · (made in code; `Fallen leaf • ochre`, `Fallen leaf • peat brown`, `MADE • straw` darkened, `PAPER • aged torn fragments`)

## Tried and taken out

- A broken flag by the stack of setts, and a heap of grit beside the stack: the stack hid both from the eye, so both were taken out (a thing nobody can see is not dressing).

## Traps

- Nothing goes in the open road or on the walking line of the pavement: the bins and the setts stand at the kerb against the lamp post.
- Nothing stands between a puddle and the lamp it mirrors; no smear goes over a puddle or the glossy setts (stains thin, off the water).
- A thing nobody can name at full size is noise: each thing is checked at 540 x 960 and taken out if it cannot be named.
- Keep this list true during the dressing: a thing that changes in the scene changes here.
