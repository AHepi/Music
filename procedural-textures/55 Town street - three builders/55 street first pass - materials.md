# Materials: the town street

A tenement street in a northern British town, about 1915, at dusk after rain. Tall 9:16 frame, eye height, looking down the street to a tenement that closes the far end, where a lit shop window and a gas lamp are the warm heart of the picture.

## Who uses this place

- **Who is here (not in the picture):** the families in the tenement flats above the shops; the dairyman and the grocer who keep the shops on the ground floor; the coal merchant who left his cart at the kerb for the night; the lamplighter who has just been round.
- **What they do all day:** carry milk, coal and water up the stairs; scrub and whiten their doorsteps; open and shut the shops; cart goods over the setts; the lamplighter lights the lamps at dusk.
- **What they left when they stepped out of frame:** the dairyman's milk churns and a crate by his door (left); the coal merchant's cart, shafts on props, against the left kerb; the grocer's barrel and crates by his shop (right); at the stair door nearest the eye (right), a besom and a bucket of mop water beside the scrubbed step, the coal scuttle on the step and a furled umbrella against the wall, with the stair's gas lit behind the door glass; the lamps lit, except the one nearest the eye, which the lamplighter has not reached; the end shop lit, its shelves of jars and tins showing; a fire lit in the end tenement (smoke from its stack).

## Traps

- Shop boards stay blank: no words anywhere.
