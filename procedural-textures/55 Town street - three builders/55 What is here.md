# What is here (log 55)

What this folder is: the record of the town street built once more "with as much careful detail" (decision 79), by three fresh builders from the painted-place plugin alone, and what the build added to the plugin (0.9.0).

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `55 Town street - first pass - final.png` | The first builder's picture (540 x 960): a northern tenement street at dusk after rain, a close, three shops with blank signs, gas lamps, a cart, setts and puddles mirroring the lamps |
| `55 Town street - second pass - final.png` | The second builder's dressing of the near ground: a relaid patch of setts, grit in the joints, rocked and missing setts, gutter silt, straw, leaves, a newspaper, the road men's stack of setts with its lantern, two ash bins |
| `55 Town street - third pass - final.png` | The third builder's work on the fronts and the middle distance: soot, damp and rust runs, hopper heads, blinds and nets, a boarded stair window, geraniums, the teapot sign, a coster barrow, a sack barrow, weathered stone |
| `55 Town street - third pass - full size.jpg` | The third pass redrawn at 1080 x 1920, 64 samples (34 minutes) |
| `55 Town street - first, second, third pass.jpg` | The three side by side |
| `55 street first pass - builder text.md`, `55 street second pass - builder text.md`, `55 street third pass - builder text.md` | The words each builder was given, and nothing else |
| `55 street first pass - builder's report.md`, `55 street second pass - builder's report.md` | The first two builders' reports, word for word |
| `55 street third pass - no report.md` | Why the third builder left no report (the machine restarted), and what stands in for it |
| `55 street first pass - materials.md`, `55 street second pass - materials.md`, `55 street third pass - materials.md` | Their material lists; the third builder's is the best account of its work |
| `55 street first pass - render_final.py`, `55 street second pass - render_final.py`, `55 street third pass - render_final.py` | Their scripts (they read the store from the builders' copy of the kit, `/home/claude/build-55-street/kit`, which exists only on this machine) |
| `55 store shop and windows helper - report.md`, `55 store street things helper - report.md`, `55 store fronts-tools helper - report.md` | Three helpers' reports on what went into the store, word for word |
| `55 store ground-tools helper - report (from notes).md` | What the fourth helper made, written from the session's notes (its own report was not saved word for word) |

The board of photographs made for the builders is in the plugin: `plugins/painted-place/brief/boards/town street/`.

## Traps

- The scripts here do not run from this folder: they need the builders' kit copy at the path written at their top.
- The third pass's final is its last finished picture, drawn just before the restart, not a picture the builder chose as final.
