Kinds pitched, lean-to or flat-topped: a slate roof, an eaves board, rafter ends that follow the slope, a half-round cast-iron gutter on brackets with outlets where asked; it carries the gables and wall head up in stone. Returns `{'roof': [one object per slope], 'eaves', 'gutters', 'walls', 'outlets': [points]}`. Options: overhang (one number, or one per side; 'shared' for a party wall, flush there), pitch, ridge ('long' / 'front' / 'side'), high_side, wall_head (0.25 m), gutters, outlets, rafters (0.6 m), seed, four materials; face read from the relief lay_stone writes (0.24 m) so the overhang is measured past the blocks.
