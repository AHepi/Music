## What happened

It started at 16:15 and stopped at 16:27, with 68 of its 80 minutes unused, because the skill allows one fix pass. Round 2 measured warm 56 percent (band 15 to 35) and cool 28 percent (band 30 to 60). Its own list of faults: the nearest hut fills the left third as a big pale timber wall; the ground reads as wet, shiny concrete with an empty bottom half; the barbed wire coils and the brazier read as plain black cylinders; the gate and sandbags fell outside the narrow frame; the weed tufts are huge beside the crates. It had a fix list ready and did not use it.

## What it shows about the plugin

- The skill's "one fix pass, then stop" made a builder stop early with a picture it knew was broken.
- The `earth` role's material (the street's road bed, made to lie under setts) reads as a mirror sheet on a big open ground.
- A simple iron cylinder reads as a black blob; small props need their own shapes.
- Nothing in the skill warns that the 32 mm tall frame is only about 3 m wide at 9 m, so a building near the camera fills a third of it.

## Traps

- Compare it with folder 46 knowing that the pictures-only builder used its full 51 minutes and drew three full-quality finals; this builder drew two pictures.
