FAILURES AND FIXES ALONG THE WAY
  First rock cutter ate 93 percent of the stone's volume (replaced by one that cuts shallow caps, 3 percent loss). Convex-hull helper crashed on a repeated geometry list (fixed).
  Shoji rails shortened by 3 mm first made the whole lattice 'float' in the gate (fixed by keeping rail ends on the posts and moving the outer uprights instead).
  First shore stones and ladle floated or were buried under the bank (fixed). First rake mesh (2.5 cm cells) made the crests zigzag between mesh columns, visible in the
  first EEVEE preview; the period is now fitted to 5 squares and the lines are exactly straight. The 'real' test file was 23 MB (over 20); basin and shore stones now use
  one subdivision level (17 MB). One shell command was refused by the safety check (a delete with a relative glob); nothing was lost and the cleanup was redone in Python.
