# Fix list: my own read of the builder previews (22 October, 07:30 UTC)

What this file is: what I (the main session) saw in the builders' preview sheets that still misses the concept art. These go into the fix
round after the workflow's own checkers, whether or not the checkers flag them. The person's rule: keep the art style, do not make it
more realistic; details are designed parts of the style.

## Ground
1. Grass tufts are thin and spiky (realistic sedge). The concept's tufts are chunky: broad blades, fat seed heads, soft rim. Make the blades wider and fewer, with plump seed heads.
2. Fallen maple leaves and the small white flowers are too small to read at the hero and veranda cameras. The concept has big, readable red leaves and white flower dots.
3. Green moss blotches on the worn earth path look like camouflage patches. The concept's bare earth is soft, with a few pebbles.
4. Several pond-side rocks show wavy contour stripes, like an agate (visible in the moss close-up and the pond close-up). This was already there before; it looks like a texture fault.

## Pond
5. Still dark. The concept has a see-through shallow edge with visible pebbles, a dark middle, big notched lily pads with pink flowers, and floating red leaves. The pads got smaller and browner.

## Trees
6. The pine's needle discs are fuzzy, like hair. The concept's are brush-stroke clumps. The maple is close to the concept: keep it.
