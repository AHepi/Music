All the bridge wood is weathered: silver-grey with a few darker boards, worn rounded edges and corners, hairline cracks, a few knots, soft dark mould patches and green moss at the plank ends and along the edges nearest the water (soft patches, not stripes), a wet dark band with green algae on the posts and stones at the waterline.
THE ROOF: the tiles vary a little in tone and size, a few chipped or slipped, soft green moss and pale yellow lichen in the valleys and along the ridge, dark drip stains below the eave.
GRASS AND GROUND: grass in clear tufts and clumps in slightly different olive shades, some with seed heads, only about a dozen tiny white and yellow flowers in the whole picture (no meadow), soft moss cushions on the rocks and around them, a few fallen maple leaves.
THE POND: clear water showing the dark stone bed, reeds and iris leaves at the edge with a few purple irises, a green algae line at the waterline, gentle ripples and reflections, round lily pads with a few pink lily flowers.
THE TREES: the maple keeps its silhouette, but trunk and branches show through gaps between the leaf clusters; the pine keeps its silhouette, with clearly separated layered needle clouds on a twisting trunk.
