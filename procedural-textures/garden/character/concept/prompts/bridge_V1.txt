THE BRIDGE keeps its zigzag route, its width and its position. Make the construction make sense: under every straight run two heavy square timber beams run along the bridge and rest on short thick posts that stand in the pond on flat stone footing blocks; the deck planks lie across the beams with narrow even gaps, fixed with visible round wooden pegs. The posts stop below the deck: NO posts, rails or poles rise above the deck. Each corner is a square landing carried by four posts.
