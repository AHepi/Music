THE BRIDGE keeps its zigzag route, its width and its position, but is built like a traditional Japanese zigzag iris-garden bridge: each straight run is laid with long wide planks running ALONG the walking direction, two or three planks side by side with a thin gap, resting on heavy cross beams that are carried by short thick posts standing in the pond on flat stones. The planks overlap by a hand-width at each corner and are fixed with dark iron cramps and wooden pegs. The posts stop below the deck: NO rails, posts or poles rise above the deck.
