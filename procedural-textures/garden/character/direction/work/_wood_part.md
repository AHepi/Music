## A. What you build (priority order; a finished step 1 plus 2 is already a valid delivery)
**Step 1 (about 60 min): the bridge, built so that it makes sense.**
Measured route (world metres): nodes N3 west bank (-5.95, 1.55), N2 landing (-3.90, 0.15), N1 landing (-2.00, 1.00), N0 east bank (-0.55, -0.60). Arms: N3 to N2 2.48 m (direction -34 degrees), N2 to N1 2.08 m (+24 degrees), N1 to N0 2.16 m (-48 degrees). Deck width 1.05 m, landings 1.2 m squares turned to bisect each turn. Water z -0.12, pond bed z -0.45 to -0.35 (ray cast onto `Life / pond bed`), ground at the west bank about z 0.08. Today: five plank runs (`Astra / varied planks.003` to `.007`) overlapping at the turns, six thin sticks (`Bridge stringers`), four small stone blocks (`Bridge pier 0` to `3`), no posts, no cross beams. Build from the bottom:
* a. FOOTING STONES: flat chunky stones (45 to 60 cm, 10 to 14 cm thick, 2 to 3 cm bevel) standing on the bed, tops about z -0.30 (under clear water, visible), one under every post. Re-use and reshape the four `Bridge pier` objects (keep their names and their `CT / Stone painterly surface.039` to `.042` materials) as footing or bank sill, and add the rest as new objects (material copies `G6 wood / footing stone N` of `.039`).
* b. POSTS: square 14 x 14 cm, 1.5 cm chamfer, in pairs just outside the planks (about 0.62 m from the centre line), at least 7 bearer lines (the 4 nodes plus the middle of each arm) so that no unsupported span is longer than 1.4 m. At the 4 nodes the posts rise 10 to 20 cm above the deck (bollards, height varies by 3 cm, chamfered tops); the mid-arm posts stop 3 to 5 cm below the plank tops. Out of plumb by up to 0.8 degrees at random. Lengths from ray casts so every post stands ON its footing within 1 cm.
* c. BEARERS (cross beams): 12 wide x 10 high, about 1.14 m long, the ends set 2 cm into notches cut in the inner faces of the post pair and pegged (2 round pegs per joint); top about z +0.04, bottom about z -0.06 (just above the water).
* d. STRINGERS (replace `Bridge stringers`): two per arm at about 0.42 m either side of the centre line (so the planks overhang them by 4 to 12 cm), three under the landings, 8 x 12 cm, lying on the bearers with half-lap joints over each bearer, tops at the plank undersides.
* e. PLANKS ACROSS: laid across the stringers; each plank overhangs the outer stringer by 4 to 12 cm (varies); each arm's planks stop 1.5 to 2.5 cm short of the landing; landing planks run across the landing; ONE diagonal breaker board (30 to 45 degrees) at each of the two turns as in `sheet_bridge.jpg`; no overlap between runs (at least 1 cm between any two planks); walking surface z 0.22 +- 0.01.
* f. FIXINGS: round wooden pegs (14 mm, 6 to 8 mm proud, domed, a lighter end-grain tone), two per plank where it crosses the outer stringers (skip about 10 percent); iron cramps (flat strap 3 x 12 cm, 4 mm thick, two nail heads, rust brown #5a3a2a with a lighter edge) across 6 to 10 plank joints at the turns and on the landings; replaced planks get small dark nail heads instead of pegs.
* g. BANK ENDS: re-use pier 0 and pier 3 as stone sills raised so their tops are about z 0.10; the first bearer line sits on them; the first plank is at least 2 cm clear of the ground.
Done when direction tests 9 and 10 pass on your staged scene and the hero picture shows posts, a bearer and a stringer under the deck edge.
**Step 2 (about 40 min): plank geometry.** Replace the plain boxes (keep the object names `Astra / varied planks.003` to `.007`; replace the mesh data; set `astra_active` false on the 8 `Astra / Planked run controls*` empties and hide them). At most 400 vertices a plank. Write point attributes `wear`, `damp`, `plank_id`, `plank_tone` for the shader. UVs: u along the length in metres, v across in metres, plus a random offset per plank. Planks have separate material slots for top, side and end faces.
**Step 3 (about 30 min): wood materials and normal maps.** New materials `G6 wood / plank top`, `/ plank side`, `/ plank end grain`, `/ post timber`, `/ iron`, `/ peg`. Colour: silvered grey-brown #9b8476 with plank-to-plank tone variation of 0.04 in luminance, replaced planks warm #8e6958, end grain darker #5c4a3e, walking line lighter by 0.02 to 0.06, mould = soft dark grey-green stains in shade, moss = soft rounded olive blobs with a lighter top edge near posts, rocks and the water and as thin strips on the side faces of the gaps; post feet dark and wet up to the waterline with a green band. Roughness 0.65 to 0.9, no gloss, no wet streaks. You may stop using the 56 `Astra / planks tone` materials (leave them in the file unused).
**Step 4 (about 30 min): the veranda deck.** Rebuild the three deck runs (`Astra / varied planks`, `.001`, `.002`): boards 12 to 17 cm wide, 4.5 to 6 cm thick, 4 to 8 mm gaps, lengths 1.2 to 2.4 m with staggered butt joints (neighbouring joints at least 40 cm apart), a 3 to 4 cm nosing at the front edge, one nail or peg head per board per joist line (joists about every 0.9 m), tones from warm to silvered, a lighter worn band 0.9 m wide in front of the shoji and at the steps, a few seams with moss at the outer edge, 5 to 8 maple leaves (new objects `G6 wood / deck leaf N`, own material copy, warm orange). Deck top stays z 0.56 +- 0.005.
**Step 5 (about 20 min): posts, beams, rafters, steps.** `Hall posts`, `Hall beams`, `Eave soffit`, `Roof carrying frame` (rafter tails): chamfer 1.5 cm, 2 or 3 vertical drying checks per post (real 2 mm grooves), darker end grain, soot on the rafter tails, posts darker and damper near the deck (material, z below 0.8). `Veranda step` and `Veranda step, lower` (stone, materials `.008` and `.009`): rounded chips on the front edge, a 5 mm worn dip on the upper step, moss at the foot, normal maps. Keep `Hall beams`, `Eave soffit` and the other timber in `G4 rim receivers`.
