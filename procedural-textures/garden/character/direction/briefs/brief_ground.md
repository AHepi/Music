Per-tuft colour by vertex colour: dark olive base (#2a2d24) to lighter olive tip (#6a6a2e); 20 percent with dry straw tips (#8a7a3a), 10 percent with a reddish autumn tint (#7a4a22); no yellow drift of the lawn.
