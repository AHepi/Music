# 36 Blind judging round 2 - scores

What this file is: how three fresh judges scored four forest villages without knowing which was which, after Opus's second pass.

**How it was done:** the same way as round 1 (log 34): 20 pictures (four villages, five views) under letters A to D, shuffled differently in
every view; three fresh judges, each seeing only those 20 files, scored every picture from 1 to 10 through one lens each (real photograph?
delivers the request? usable film backdrop?). The letters were decoded afterwards with `which letter was which village.json`.

The scores of the same pictures moved between rounds (Sonnet 6.3 to 5.6, Opus's first pass 6.4 to 6.0, the plugin village 2.7 both times):
judges score against what else is on the table, and the second pass raised the bar.

## What they said about the second pass

- **Strengths:** "the most photographic of the set": low golden sun, wet ruts with a puddle that truly reflects the sky, grass growing in the
  road's centre, atmospheric depth to the far cottages; weathered silver-grey timber and moss on the stable shingles against real-looking
  spruces; very convincing meadow grass; from above it "reads like a drone photo".
- **Still wrong:** everything too clean (spotless plaster, new fences, trough and rails, no mud splash at wall feet, no clutter, dung or straw);
  the thatch reads as "scaly shingles or clay" with a padded eave band; the stable-yard puddles are "flat black patches that read as holes";
  the road close-up is "soft, blurry beach sand" with smooth sculpted ruts and almost no stones, twigs or hoof prints; from above the ruts are
  still "two thin parallel lines, like tram rails", the lawn is mown and even, the haystacks are perfect domes, and two distant cottages are
  identical copies.
- **Their shared advice for every village:** vary the scatter, add mud and weathering at the base of every building, add working clutter and
  smoke, fix the puddles, and break up cloned trees and houses.
