Another village in the set scored higher on delivering the request (7.3), mainly because its roads were the muddiest, wettest and weediest:
dark wet mud, puddles along the ruts, a grass crown down the middle, twigs and leaves. It had no hoof prints at all, which yours did.

## Keep (the judges liked these)

- The dense spruce and broadleaf forest, which "reads like real aerial drone footage", and the sky with clouds.
- The tall dry meadow grass ("very photographic").
- The hoof prints down the middle of the road, the curved and branching turning ruts, the puddle that reflects the sky.
- The stable: "the most believable empty stable": open front, empty stalls, half doors, hitching rail, bucket, trough, dung, cart, mossy roof.
- The cottage's mediaeval detail: half-timbering with braces, thick thatch, stone chimney, plank shutters, barred window, bench, pot, barrel, wattle fence.

## Fix (the judges' words, grouped)

**Roads and ground (the biggest gap):**
- "The road is a too-smooth, too-clean sand plane", "like plaster or beach sand", "concrete dust", "packed sand, with little real mud": no fine grit,
  no organic debris, no wetness except the puddle. It needs real dirt: darker damp earth, mud in and beside the ruts, wet darkening, grit, straw,
  leaves, twigs, dung.
- From above, the roads are "pale, flat ribbons with perfectly parallel, evenly spaced rut lines and razor-clean edges" that "read as railway or
  tram tracks"; they "look kept, not unkept"; "no mud, puddles or weeds show in the track".
- "Smooth grey-green berms" / "a hard grey-green band where the verge meets the road" / "a sandy slab with a hard lip where the grass is stuck in":
  the road edge needs a ragged, soft transition with grass and weeds growing into it.
- The ruts are "soft, rounded grooves with a dark smear", "a smooth, uniform profile like tyre tracks"; in the eye-level view the nearest ruts are
  dry and shallow (your own note).
- "The footprints look stamped on" / "stamped in".
- Pebbles are "evenly sprinkled", "like polka dots", "round green-grey"; scatter must be random and clumped, with varied sizes and colours,
  partly sunk into the ground.

**Puddles:**
- "A flat, over-saturated blue patch like paint", "a hard-edged, over-saturated blue cut-out", "pasted on".
- In the stable view: "an odd pure-black hole shape in the foreground puddle" that "reads as a hole in the ground".
- Water should be murky brown, reflect the real surroundings, have soft edges with darker wet ground around them.

**Clearing and layout (from above):**
- "An even dark-green lawn that looks mown, like a golf course, with no gardens, fields, woodpiles or bare yards"; "the houses are tiny, spotless
  and few for the size of the clearing"; "no building clearly reads as the stable, and the props are too small to read".
- "The lone birches are thin and sparse, like planted saplings", "stick props that look pasted on".
- "Three hay mounds are smooth egg-shaped blobs"; "a round domed object at bottom centre is half hidden by a conifer and reads as a mistake".
- "The light is soft and flat, with little mood."

**Buildings and props:**
- Thatch: "a smoothly streaked, brushed texture with a sausage-like eave", "looks like motion-blurred fur", "soft, mushy, uniform".
- "Stray straw strands float in front of the walls like fuzz or sparkles", "hang down across the white walls like scratches or hairs".
- Plaster, timber, boards and barrel are "too clean and new", "clean and unweathered": they need stains, damp and rot at the base, moss,
  weathering, worn edges.
- "The gable end is clad in modern-looking dark weatherboard."
- "The shutters are flat, and the dark window recess is uniform black."
- Stable roof: "very evenly tiled with flat moss patches", "blotchy flat spotting".
- "A dark, blurry shadow sits under the rail."
- The haystack is "a smooth dome blob".
- "Uncut grass runs right up to the walls with no trodden ground at the door, so it reads as abandoned": add worn paths and trodden earth at doors,
  gates and the well, and mud, straw and dung where people and horses walk.
- You called the hidden fill light inside the stable a cheat; light the inside from a real source if you can.

**Composition and light:**
- Eye level: "the cottage is awkwardly cut by the left frame edge, along with a stray fence post"; "the tree line is a dark, monotonous wall";
  "the light is cool and flat".
- Cottage: "the roof is cut at top left and the barrel at bottom left, which looks unplanned".
- Stable: "the frontal, roof-cropped composition and the large empty yard are static".
- Road close-up: "the overall grade is greyish and flat, with little warmth".
