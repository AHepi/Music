# 34 Blind judging - scores

What this file is: how three fresh judges scored the three forest villages without knowing which was which, and what they said.

**How it was done:** the 15 pictures (three villages, five views) were copied under letters A, B and C, shuffled differently in every view. Three
judges, each a fresh helper that saw only those 15 files, scored every picture from 1 to 10 through one lens each: would it pass as a real photograph;
does it deliver the request (unkept dirt roads worn by horses and carts, an empty stable, a few cottages, mediaeval props, in a forest); would an art
director use it as a film backdrop. The letters were decoded afterwards with `which letter was which village.json`.

## Scores (out of 10, mean of the five views)

| | Real photograph? | Delivers the request? | Usable film backdrop? | All three |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| With the plugin (Sonnet) | 1.9 | 3.5 | 2.6 | **2.7** |
| No plugin: Sonnet, maximum effort | 5.4 | 7.3 | 6.2 | **6.3** |
| No plugin: Opus, maximum effort | 6.0 | 6.9 | 6.2 | **6.4** |

Per view (mean of the three lenses): from above, Opus 6.2, Sonnet 5.5, plugin 2.8; road at eye level, Opus 6.7, Sonnet 6.7, plugin 2.7; stable,
Opus 6.2, Sonnet 6.2, plugin 2.5; cottage, Sonnet 6.8, Opus 6.7, plugin 3.3; road close-up, Sonnet 6.3, Opus 6.2, plugin 2.0.

Best picture per view: Opus 7 times out of 15, Sonnet 8, the plugin village never. The realism judge picked Opus in four of five views; the request
judge picked Sonnet in four of five.

## What they said, in short

- **The plugin village** was recognised in every view as "a low-poly game render": faceted trees, a flat green backdrop wall, an empty sky,
  stretched and smeared ground pictures, evenly spaced lone sprouts. The film judge: it "would need a full rebuild".
- **Both no-plugin villages** share the same remaining faults: surfaces too clean (sand-like roads, clay-like mud, new wood and plaster), pebbles
  scattered evenly like sweets, striped conifer cards and flat leaf cards, smooth blob haystacks, thatch that looks brushed, puddles like flat
  paint or a black hole, and too little mud, straw and dung where people and animals walk.
- **Sonnet's** strengths: the muddiest, wettest, weediest roads with a grass crown and twigs; warm, lived-in scenes (log pile, flowers, fences).
  Its gap: no hoof prints visible anywhere.
- **Opus's** strengths: the most photographic forest, sky with clouds and tall meadow grass; the only hoof prints and turning cart tracks; the
  best stable (empty stalls, hitching rail, dung, tracks). Its gaps: a clean, pale, sand-like road, puddles like blue paint, a black hole in the
  stable yard, floating straw strands round the thatch.
- **The judges' advice for all:** dirtier, weathered, irregular surfaces; random rather than even scatter; better tree leaves and needles; softer
  puddle edges with darker wet ground round them; mud, straw and dung where feet and hooves go.
