# Materials: a British army camp, late 1915, dusk after rain

Who is here (not in the picture): a battery's gunners and the camp's pioneers, a few dozen men, wet through, tired.
What they do all day: haul and lay duckboards over churned ground, string wire, stack shell boxes, clean the gun, carry water and tea between the hut and the tents.
What they left when they stepped out of frame: a lit hurricane lantern on a crate by the walkway, a stack of shell boxes beside the gun, a bucket by the tent door, a barrel by the hut step, sandbags piled where the gunners shelter, a coil of wire dropped at the fence's end, lamplight in the hut windows.
