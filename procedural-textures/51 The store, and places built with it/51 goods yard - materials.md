# Materials: a railway goods yard at dusk after rain (Britain, about 1915)

Three lines first.
- **Who is here** (they are not in the picture): the yard foreman and his two porters, a carter with his dray, the lamplighter, the signalman, the shunter.
- **What they do all day**: unload wagons of coal and timber, truck crates and barrels from the wagons over the platform into the shed (and back), weigh and chalk consignments, shunt wagons with a pole, light the lamp at dusk.
- **What they left when they stepped out of frame**: a hurricane lantern on a crate by the shed door, a hand trolley left loaded beside the platform edge, barrels stood up in a row against the shed, sacks of coal slumped by the wagon, crates stacked where the carter will fetch them, a shunter's pole on the ballast, loose sleepers stacked beside the siding, the shed door left ajar with the office lamp lit, the gas lamp lit.
