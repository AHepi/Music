# Changes from the old brief to the new brief

## finish (WET)

OLD: simplified painterly textures on brick, stone, slate and wood, and matte painted finish.

NEW: simplified painterly textures on brick, stone, slate and wood, and painted wet shine and painted reflections.

## wet street (WET)

OLD: It is a pit-village street at dusk on 1 May 1926.

NEW: It is a pit-village street at dusk on 1 May 1926, wet after rain.

## props kept (PROPS)

OLD: the empty old wooden kitchen chair with worn, grubby pale paint, darker than the notice, its back to the board, with a small hurricane lantern on its seat (amber glass, a small flame inside); the wooden pail at the chair's feet, with no handle; the dark timber notice board on its two posts with the cream notice (plain paper with grey rows of print-like marks, no readable letters); the black gas lamp standing free on its own post above the board; the brick terraces, the colliery building, its tall chimney and winding wheels; and far up on the left the small two-wheeled handcart seen from behind, the coal sacks and the dustbins.

NEW: the dark timber notice board on its two posts with the cream notice (plain paper with grey rows of print-like marks, no readable letters); the small wooden pail at the foot of the board, with no handle; the black gas lamps on their posts down both sides of the street; the brick terraces and stone cottages, the colliery building, its tall chimney and winding wheels; the wooden handcart on the left with its big spoked wheel and the spade leaning on it; the pile of coal sacks in the road; the small heap of black coal lumps on the road; the dustbin at the right edge; and the ladder on the left pavement.

## gutter (WET)

OLD: The gutter beside the kerb is a shallow, smooth stone channel with no steps, ridges or grating.

NEW: The gutter beside the kerb is a shallow, smooth stone channel with no steps, ridges or grating, with the rainwater lying in it as image 1 shows.

## colour and wet paragraph (WET)

OLD: The road, pavement and walls are cool blue-grey dusk, and the brick is weathered and greyed by coal smoke without getting any darker. Warm light stays only where it already is: round the drum, on the board, on the chair seat, at the lamps and in the few lit windows.

NEW: The road, pavement and walls are cool blue-grey dusk, and the brick is weathered and greyed by coal smoke without getting any darker. The street is wet after rain, and image 1 shows exactly where. Keep all of that wet look exactly where image 1 has it, at the same size and the same strength, painted in the hand of image 2 (painted reflections made of flat shapes of colour with a few ink lines and visible brush strokes; not a photograph, not a mirror): the wet sheen on the setts of the road and on the pavement flags; the lamp, lit-window and brazier reflections lying on the road as soft painted streaks and pools of warm light; the soft reflections of the handcart and the sacks on the road under them; the puddles in the gutters and in the low spots of the road, each one a smooth painted sheet of cool grey-blue with the houses, lamps and lit windows mirrored upside down in it; the thin dark wet lines between the setts and between the flags; and the darker, damper foot of the walls. The road is darker and glossier than a dry road, and never looks dry. Add no wet look where image 1 has none. Warm light stays only where it already is: round the drum, on the board, at the lamps, in the few lit windows, and in their reflections on the wet road where image 1 shows them.

## thin parts (PROPS)

OLD: (the lantern's wire handle, the lamp arms, the gate bars, the chair rungs, the winding rope)

NEW: (the lamp arms, the gate bars, the cart's rails and spokes, the spade's handle, the winding rope)

## touching pairs (PROPS)

OLD: the drum's side and the board's left post, the gas-lamp head and the wall pipe and bracket beside it, the far lamps and the house downpipes, the chair back across the bottom of the notice; the winding rope never joins the lamp.

NEW: the drum's side and the board's left post, each gas-lamp head and the wall pipe and bracket beside it, the far lamps and the house downpipes, the spade and the cart's wheel; the winding rope never joins the lamp.

## never add halos (WET)

OLD: glows, halos or light rays round any light, the notice or the drum;

NEW: glows, halos or light rays round any light, the notice or the drum (the wet road's reflections of the lights are not halos: keep those as image 1 has them);

## never add puddles (WET)

OLD: puddles or reflections; a moon,

NEW: a moon,
