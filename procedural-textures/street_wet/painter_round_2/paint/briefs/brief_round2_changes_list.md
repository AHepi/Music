# Changes from the first test's brief to the round 2 brief

## lamp glass

Why: the person asked: the gas lamp glass glows warm amber (the first painting made it pale yellow-white)

OLD: Every gas lamp has warm amber glass with a small bright gas mantle inside.

NEW: Every gas lamp has warm amber glass with a small bright gas mantle inside, and the gas lamp glass glows warm amber.

## sky

Why: the first painting added heavy pale-white brush-stroke clouds to a plain dark sky although the never-add list banned bright clouds

OLD: The road, pavement and walls are cool blue-grey dusk, and the brick is weathered and greyed by coal smoke without getting any darker. 

NEW: The road, pavement and walls are cool blue-grey dusk, and the brick is weathered and greyed by coal smoke without getting any darker. The sky is dark grey-blue cloud with soft edges, like image 2's darker skies; no pale, white or bright clouds anywhere; keep the sky as dark as image 1. 

## streaks

Why: the first painting made the far lamp reflection streaks on the road much stronger than the plate had them

OLD: Add no wet look where image 1 has none. 

NEW: Add no wet look where image 1 has none. Do not make the lamp reflection streaks on the road stronger or longer than in image 1. 

## joint lines

Why: keep the thin dark joint lines under every light (the person asked that the stonework stays visible)

OLD: Add no wet look where image 1 has none. Do not make

NEW: Add no wet look where image 1 has none. Keep the thin dark lines between the setts and between the flags everywhere, even where the road shines. Do not make

## rubbish kept

Why: the plate now has small rubbish in the gutters and along the walls; the painter must keep it and invent no writing or modern litter

OLD: Keep: the rusty drum

NEW: Keep the small rubbish where image 1 has it (cinders, coal dust, ash, paper scraps, straw, leaves, mud along the gutters and the foot of the walls, round the drum and sacks); add no writing, print or modern litter. Keep: the rusty drum

## never add: litter

Why: the first brief said "no ... litter in the road or on the pavement", which would now fight the sentence that keeps the plate's small rubbish

OLD: extra props (no coal tub, rails, barrel, crates or litter in the road or on the pavement)

NEW: extra props (no coal tub, rails, barrel or crates in the road or on the pavement)

## never add: clouds

Why: say the same thing in the never-add list as in the sky sentence

OLD: a moon, stars or bright clouds;

NEW: a moon, stars or any pale, white or bright clouds;

## halo sentence

Why: the lamp glass is now asked to glow amber, so say that this glow is not the banned halo

OLD: (the wet road's reflections of the lights are not halos: keep those as image 1 has them)

NEW: (the wet road's reflections of the lights, and the warm amber of the lamp glass itself, are not halos: keep those as image 1 has them)
