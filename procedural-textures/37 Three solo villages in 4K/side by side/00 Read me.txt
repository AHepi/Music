THREE SOLO VILLAGES IN 4K - SIDE BY SIDE (log 37)

What this folder is: the three forest villages that were built without the plugin,
each drawn at 4K (3840 x 2560 pixels per picture) on rented graphics cards,
from 11 views, and put side by side two at a time.

THE THREE VILLAGES
- Sonnet alone: Sonnet at maximum effort, no plugin (log 33).
- Opus alone, first pass: Opus at maximum effort, no plugin (log 34).
- Opus alone, second pass: Opus's second go, working from the judges' notes (log 36).

THE 11 VIEWS
01 Whole village from above        07 Props close
02 Road at eye level               08 Into the empty stable stalls
03 Stable close                    09 Puddle at ground level
04 Cottage close                   10 Road into the forest
05 Road surface close-up           11 Village from a low drone height
06 A second cottage

Views 01 to 05 are the five views each village was judged on, using that
village's own cameras. Views 06 to 11 are new: a camera was placed in each
village to show the same kind of subject.

THE THREE PAIRINGS (each view has all three)
- Sonnet alone (left) vs Opus alone, second pass (right)
- Opus alone, first pass (left) vs Opus alone, second pass (right)
- Sonnet alone (left) vs Opus alone, first pass (right)

GOOD TO KNOW
- Each village keeps its own light, brightness and settings from its own final
  pictures, so these match what the judges saw, only sharper.
- Sonnet's and Opus's villages are laid out differently, so their pictures of
  "a second cottage" or "the props" show different spots, not the same spot.
- Opus's two passes share one layout. Their new views 06 to 11 use the very
  same cameras, so those pairs are exact before-and-after pictures. Views 01
  to 05 are slightly reframed in the second pass (Opus improved its framing).
- Each side of a pair is a full 4K picture. Zoom in: the detail is there.
