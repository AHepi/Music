**Verdict: no.**
It misses most points of the look at the same time. The lower half is bright and warm when it should be dark, nothing looks old, the air is empty, and the fire looks like a toy. The fixes below rebuild most of the picture rather than adjust it. More is wrong than seven fixes cover: the modern box lights on the porch, the flat saw-toothed palm leaves, and the post cut off at the right edge.

**Fixes, biggest part of the picture first**

1. **DIAL.** Sees: the whole lower half (sand, porch and front wall) glows an even warm beige, and no shadow below the roofline is blue. Even the bottom corners, far from any light, stay warm brown.
   Change: make the fire the one strong light, with a small amber pool round it and only a dim spill from the windows onto the porch and steps, and let the rest of the sand fall into blue-black shadow.

2. **SURFACE.** Sees: the sand is a smooth, shiny sheet like a polished floor. The round glare on the left and the two bright streaks below the fire are reflections in it.
   Change: make it dry, dull, grainy sand with ripples, footprints and drag marks, which takes the light softly and reflects nothing.

3. **SHAPE.** Sees: the bottom third of the frame is bare sand, and nothing leads the eye from the bottom edge into the picture.
   Change: run a line from the bottom centre up to the porch steps (a worn plank walk, or a path edged with stones) and crowd the bottom edge with used things: driftwood, a coiled rope, a bucket, a torn net, a lantern, shells.

4. **SURFACE.** Sees: everything is clean and new: the hut's pale planks, door and chairs, the roof in one even orange, and the canoe on the right.
   Change: age all of it: grey weathered wood with water stains and flaking paint, rust streaks running down from the roof edge, grime and drifted sand in the corners, and a scuffed, patched canoe.

5. **DIAL.** Sees: the air is empty. The palms behind the hut are near-black and sharp-edged, darker than the hut in front of them, and no glow hangs in the air round the porch or the fire.
   Change: add haze so the palms and everything behind the hut turn paler and bluer the farther away they are, with a soft glow in the air round the porch and the fire.

6. **SHAPE.** Sees: the campfire in the lower middle is a glowing white cone in a ring of dice-shaped stones, with a pencil-shaped log lying above it, and it gives off no smoke.
   Change: build a real fire, with ragged amber flames over charred, broken logs and red embers, a ring of rough uneven stones, a split log instead of the pencil, and a thin, ragged trail of smoke lit orange from below.

7. **SHAPE.** Sees: behind the hut on the right, between the porch and the canoe, a large smooth dark shape with no detail stands against the sky, and there is no telling what it is.
   Change: make it something a viewer can name (a boat pulled up on its side, a cracked rock with weed on it, a stack of barrels and nets), or take it out.

**The numbers against my eyes**
The numbers agree it is far too warm. 61 percent of the frame is warm, and the most the band allows is 35. Bright warm light covers 15 percent of the frame, and the most the band allows is 3.

The numbers call brightness "inside" (0.31). My eyes disagree, and I trust my eyes. Brightness only passes because the dark sky balances out the lower half, and the lower half alone is far brighter than the band allows. The amount of cool colour also passes only because of the sky.

Most of the warmth is in the sand, which stays brown even in the corners far from any light. So turning the window light down, as the numbers suggest, will not fix it by itself.

**What already works**
Keep the tall frame, the eye at standing height, and the hut as the main subject near the centre. Its size comes from the door, chairs, steps and canoe, with no people in frame. Keep the deep teal-blue sky, the amber glow through the slats of the left-side windows, and the rust-orange of the roof, which sits between those two colours. The smoothed finish, crisp on the hut and soft in the sky, matches the coal mine render.
