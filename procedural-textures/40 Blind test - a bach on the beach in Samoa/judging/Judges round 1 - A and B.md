# Judges, round 1: pictures A and B

Three fresh judges (Opus), each given only the judging folder (pictures A and B at 1080 x 1920, the five reference pictures, The look.md, the question sheet). Key, hidden from them: A = the control build (Blender alone), B = the plugin build (round 2, full size). Their answers, unedited.

## Judge 1

| Picture | Photograph | Request | Film backdrop in the look |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 4 | 7 | 1 |
| B | 2 | 5 | 5 |

Choice: B. B already has the series' light and colour: a dark blue night where a little warm light does real work, glowing through the window slats, pooling on the wall under the porch lamp and spilling onto the sand, on a weathered board house that looks lived in. A is a bright, clean, pastel daytime picture with a shiny blue boat and spotless walls, so it breaks nearly every part of the look and would have to be rebuilt starting from the light. A shows the request better (open sea, an outrigger canoe, solar panels), but B's worst faults sit in a few objects at the front that can be swapped out, while A's faults run through the whole picture.
Fix A: Turn the daytime into dusk or night, so most of the picture sits in deep blue shadow and the only warm light comes from the house's windows and its string of lights.
Fix B: Take out the black star of sticks and the round ring at the front, and replace the white spiky cones with a small, low fire of real-looking logs and flames, so the lit house stays the brightest thing in the picture.

## Judge 2

| Picture | Photograph | Request | Film backdrop in the look |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 4 | 7 | 2 |
| B | 3 | 5 | 5 |

Choice: B. It already has the series' light: a dark blue night where the warm lit windows and the porch lamp are the only real light, throwing a pool of light on the wall and catching the smoke, so it could sit beside the coal mine and the rainy street. A is a bright, clean, pastel daylight scene with brand-new paint and a plastic-looking boat, so its light, colour and wear would all have to be redone before it belonged. B's worst faults are a few toy-like objects at the front and a sea you cannot see, and those are smaller jobs than remaking the whole picture.
Fix A: Make it evening: darken the sky to a deep blue and switch on warm lights inside the house and on the porch, so the glowing windows become the main light in the picture.
Fix B: Swap the glowing white spikes, the ring shape and the star of black sticks at the front for a small, real driftwood fire and ordinary beach things like a coconut or a coil of rope, so the front of the picture stops looking like a toy.

## Judge 3

| Picture | Photograph | Request | Film backdrop in the look |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | 4 | 7 | 2 |
| B | 3 | 5 | 5 |

Choice: B. B already belongs to the world of the reference pictures: a dark blue night where the only warm light comes from the glowing louvre windows and a verandah lamp that throws a wedge of light across the wall, with haze in the air and rust on the roof edge. A is a bright, clean, pastel afternoon that would need new light and ageing from top to bottom to sit beside them, even though it shows the sea and the modern holiday details (surfboards, chilly bin, plastic chair) far more clearly. B's worst faults are a few objects at the front (a spiky white fire, a starburst of black sticks and a ring) that can be swapped out, so B is the shorter road to a usable backdrop.
Fix A: Turn the bright afternoon into a deep blue evening, so most of the picture sits in shadow and the only warm light comes from the house's windows and the string of bulbs along the verandah.
Fix B: Take out the spiky white fire, the black starburst of sticks and the ring at the front, and put in a small, low fire of real driftwood logs whose glow reaches only the nearest sand, so the bottom of the picture falls back into shadow.
