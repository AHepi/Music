# Materials for the dressing (second pass): the Japanese garden after rain, at dusk

What this file is: the first deliverable of the second pass. It lists only what this pass adds or changes; everything else is as the first builder's list says (`first pass/materials.md`). Written before dressing and kept true while dressing.

The owner's words: "Now try to do some of this. The animals are unnecessary." With the second picture: "Give this picture." And: "Please remind the builders to use PBR wherever possible. It save in space and compute." So: the close view of the path should look like the second picture (rough wet slabs, moss and grit in the joints, deep red leaves, sky in the water), and every surface uses the shelf's photographed sets where it can.

## Who uses this place

- **Who is here (not in the picture):** the inn's maid who keeps the garden; the guests walking to the bridge after their bath.
- **What they do all day:** she sweeps the fallen leaves off the path into the beds, carries water from the bamboo spout in a wooden bucket, wipes the veranda; the guests leave their clogs on the step stone and walk the path in borrowed ones.
- **What they left when they stepped out of frame:** her bamboo broom leant on the garden fence beside the path, and the heap of leaves she swept off the path at its edge; her wooden bucket with a bamboo dipper set down beside the path, on her way from the basin to the jar; the guest's clogs, a neat pair on the step stone, toes to the garden.

## Traps

- Nothing goes on the path itself or in the open walk to the bridge; the broom and bucket stand in the bed beside the path, against the fence.
- No smear on the glossy path or over a puddle: it kills the shine. Stains only on the matte garden soil.
- The broom must not cross the stone basin or the spout (the basin group is a key shape in the picture).
- Each added thing must be big enough to name at 540 x 960: check the bottom third at full size after every picture.
