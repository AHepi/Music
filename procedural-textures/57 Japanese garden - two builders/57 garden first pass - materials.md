# Materials: a Japanese garden after rain, beside a timber inn, at dusk

The owner's picture (kit/request) shows a wet stone path along an inn's veranda, a pond with water lilies, a red arched bridge, a stone lantern, a bamboo spout into a stone basin, a bamboo fence, maples, pines, bamboo, the inn with lit paper windows, and misty wooded mountains. The animals (the koi) are left out, as asked. Materials and use are taken from the board's photographs (kit/boards/japanese garden); light and colour from kit/reference.

## Who uses this place

- **Who is here (not in the picture):** the inn's maid who keeps the garden and the veranda, and the guests who walk the path to the bridge after their bath.
- **What they do all day:** she sweeps the path and the veranda, lights the hanging lanterns and the stone lantern at dusk, fills the jar by the veranda from the bamboo spout, wipes the veranda boards; guests walk the stepping stones and stand on the bridge.
- **What they left when they stepped out of frame:** the lanterns lit (she has just done her round), a bamboo ladle laid across the stone basin, the big blue-and-white water jar by the veranda steps, a pair of wooden clogs (geta) on the stone step below the veranda; the maple leaves the rain brought down are not yet swept.

## Traps

- The photographs are dry daylight; everything here is wet dusk: stone and timber darker, moss greener, glossy on top faces.
- A lamp inside a closed case lights nothing: the hanging lantern's and stone lantern's panes must let light out (no shadow from the panes).
- Each lit paper screen needs its own small warm light outside it, or the ground in front stays dark.
- A thing nobody can name at 540 x 960 is noise: leaves, clogs and the ladle must be big enough and lit, or go.
