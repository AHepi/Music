# What is here (log 56)

What this folder is: the record of the owner's question about the town street (decision 80: photographed stone and brick, the road on any curve, a wall that fills one plane) and the timber frames that followed (decision 81), and what they added to the plugin (0.10.0).

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `56 Curved corner after rain - timber frames.jpg` | The worked example as it now stands (540 x 960): a wet sett road that climbs and bends, kerbs and flags along both edges, a wall of flat planes along the bend (red brick, dark Flemish brick, stone blocks), a house of photographed sandstone with timber door and window frames and two lit windows, a gas lamp in a puddle |
| `56 Curved corner after rain - first final, stone frames.jpg` | The example helper's own final (19:08), before the timber frames |
| `56 cards - photographed materials.jpg` | The new materials' cards: two brick walls and sandstone blocks where every brick or block is a real one from the photographs, dark rock for setts, sandstone for flags, mortar |
| `56 cards - road and kerb on a curve.jpg` | The road tool's and kerb tool's cards: setts round an S-bend with flags and kerbs |
| `56 cards - wall on a plane.jpg` | The wall tool's card: Flemish brick beside stone blocks |
| `56 cards - first wall, and the house front.jpg` | The wall tool's first card and the house-front tool in photographed sandstone, before the timber frames |
| `56 helpers - shared text.md` and `56 materials / road / wall helper - text.md` | The words the three store helpers were given |
| `56 store materials / road / wall / frames helper - report.md`, `56 example helper - report.md` | The five helpers' reports, word for word |

The example's script is in the plugin: `plugins/painted-place/brief/store/examples/street - a curved corner after rain.py`, with its picture beside it.

## Traps

- The example's picture beside its script is the timber-frame redraw (the session's, 19:21 to 19:26); the example helper's own last picture had stone frames.
- The materials helper's report says the sandstone reads orange-tan beside the street's old honey-grey; it was not changed (lesson 237).
