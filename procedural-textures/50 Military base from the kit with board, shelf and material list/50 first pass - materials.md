# Materials: a British army camp at dusk after rain, 1915

Who is here (not in the picture): a gun battery's men and the camp guard, billeted in bell tents along a duckboard track to a timber watch tower.
What they do all day: drill, haul shells and boxes to the gun, stand watch on the tower, dig, mend wire, cook, wait out the rain.
What they left behind when they stepped out of frame: a hurricane lantern on a crate by the first tent door (lit for the evening), a bucket and a mess tin beside it, a second lantern on a box by the gun, a lamp hanging inside the first tent, a field gun with its shells and ammunition boxes beside it, a half-built sandbag wall on the left with a spade stuck in it, a coil of barbed wire and a few spare stakes where the wire party stopped, two oil drums and ammunition crates stacked beside the track, a telegraph line down the right side, loose planks and stones in the mud.

## Traps

- A lantern with its light inside its glass lights nothing; the light sits just outside the chimney.
- The tall frame is narrow near the camera; check what the camera sees before dressing.
- Wet ground gone pale or black: judge it beside the brightest lamp.
