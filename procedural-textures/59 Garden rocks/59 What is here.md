# What is here (log 59)

What this folder is: the record of the Japanese garden's rocks redone at the owner's word (decision 89): a helper made the store's `rocks` tool (rocks made by rules, scattered by Blender's particle system, in new photographed rock surfaces), a fresh builder then redid every rock in the garden with the plugin alone, and a second helper pulled what that builder made by hand into the tool (plugin 0.13.0). The garden's earlier records are the folders `57 Japanese garden - two builders` and `58 Garden closer to the picture`. The owner's pictures are not here.
