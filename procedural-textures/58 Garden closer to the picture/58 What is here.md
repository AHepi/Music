# What is here (log 58)

What this folder is: the record of the Japanese garden brought closer to the owner's first picture (decision 86): a third fresh builder compared the garden with the picture and closed the biggest differences, and two store helpers then added what it made by hand to the plugin (0.12.0). The garden's earlier record is the folder `57 Japanese garden - two builders`. The owner's two pictures are not here, and neither are the builder's comparison pictures, which hold cuts of them: they stay off GitHub, on this machine only (`/home/claude/build-57-garden/kit/request/` and `/home/claude/build-57-garden/closer/pics/`).
