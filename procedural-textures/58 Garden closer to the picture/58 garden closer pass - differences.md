# Differences: the owner's picture against the place as the earlier builders left it

What this file is: every difference I can see between the owner's picture (`kit/request/the owner's picture.png`, and the second one for the close view) and the earlier builders' pictures (`earlier/final.png`, `earlier/closeup.png`), biggest first, in the order the brief asks: the light and the sky, the big shapes and where they stand, the middle distance, the near ground, the colour of the whole. Written at 04:53 before changing anything. "Left", "right", "top" are places in the frame. The owner's picture is wider (4 by 5) than ours (9 by 16), so only what fits a tall frame is asked of ours.

## 1. The light and the sky

1. **Where the light comes from.** Owner's: a low sun just behind the mountains, a little left of the middle, straight ahead of the eye; the whole picture is lit from behind (the roofs, the maple and the bamboo glow at their edges). Ours: the sun is also low and ahead, but nothing in the picture shows it: no glow, no lit edges; the place reads as lit by blue dusk and the lamps.
2. **The sky's colour.** Owner's: a golden-white glow low in the middle behind the far ridge, broken clouds above it lit gold and peach underneath, grey-blue clouds higher up, small patches of pale blue. Ours: an even grey-blue sky with a few white clouds at the top, no warm glow anywhere in it.
3. **Sun shafts.** Owner's: clear shafts of light slant down from the upper left through the mist, over the left part of the inn into the courtyard. Ours: none.
4. **The air.** Owner's: a warm, golden mist between the inn and the mountains, thicker low down, the far ridges fading into it in layers. Ours: a thin, cold blue-grey veil; the mountains read as flat pale cut-outs against it.
5. **How bright.** Owner's: 0.25, a picture of evening with plenty of light in the middle distance. Ours: 0.18, darker, mostly shadow.

## 2. The big shapes and where they stand

6. **The mountains.** Owner's: wooded ridges in several layers, the nearest dark with tree tops on its skyline, the farther ones paler and lost in mist, filling the top middle behind the inn's roof. Ours: two bare, smooth, pale cones, the same flat colour all over.
7. **The pond.** Owner's: a big pond fills the middle of the picture from the bridge down nearly to the bottom right, mirroring the golden sky, the lit windows and the bridge; lily pads with pink flowers on it. Ours: the pond is almost hidden behind the stone basin and the rocks; only a small patch shows under the bridge.
8. **The maple.** Owner's: a big red maple hangs over the top of the picture from the top middle, its leaves lit from behind (glowing red-orange), the sky showing between its sprays. Ours: a red maple crown at the top right corner only, dark.
9. **The inn.** Owner's: a wide two-storey inn behind the pond with a great tiled hip-and-gable roof whose ridge rises to the right of the middle; rows of lit paper windows on both floors, its roof tiles pale grey where they catch the sky. Ours: the inn is in the right place but narrow, with one big bright window upstairs and a few below; its roof is near black.
10. **The garden pines.** Owner's: pruned pines with flat cloud pads of dark green stand beside the inn, left and right, and lean over the right-hand side. Ours: none in view (red maples stand there instead).
11. **The bamboo.** Owner's: bamboo at the top right with fans of green leaves against the sky. Ours: pale yellow culms only, no leaves in view.
12. **The veranda and its lantern.** Owner's: the veranda's eave across the top left, a red post with paint flaking to grey at the left edge, a big lit hanging lantern near the eye, the boards wet and shining with its light, lit paper screens going back. Ours: the same things in the same places; our post and boards are darker, the eave's underside near black, the lantern smaller.
13. **The stone lantern.** Owner's: a stout, chunky granite lantern with a wide mossy cap on big mossy rocks at the water, right of the middle, lit warm. Ours: a lit lantern there too, on a slim post, reading thin and small.

## 3. The middle distance

14. **Moss.** Owner's: bright green moss thick on every rock and on the lantern's foot, the green one of the main colours of the picture. Ours: dark rocks, the moss hardly shows.
15. **Flowering shrubs.** Owner's: a pink azalea on the left bank and a white flowering shrub on the right. Ours: none.
16. **Rocks round the pond.** Owner's: big rounded grey boulders with moss, many of them, edging the water. Ours: fewer, dark, mostly lost in shadow.
17. **Steps and a second lantern.** Owner's: stone steps going up beyond the bridge, a second stone lantern on the far left bank. Ours: none.
18. **Lit windows reflected in the pond.** Owner's: the inn's windows and the sky draw long reflections in the water. Ours: one reflection under the bridge.

## 4. The near ground

19. **The puddles' colour.** Owner's (both pictures): the water in the slabs mirrors a golden and white cloudy sky with blue in it. Ours: the puddles mirror plain blue and read pale blue-white.
20. **The slabs.** Owner's second picture: big rough dark grey-brown slabs, wet all over with fine glints, close joints with moss and grit. Ours (close view): the slabs read rounded and smooth like soft toys, the joints wide and pale beige like mortar.
21. **The fallen leaves.** Owner's: deep red and red-orange maple leaves, fewer, crisp, with pointed lobes. Ours: many leaves in yellow, orange and red, reading as flat paper cut-outs, many of them yellow.
22. **Plants at the bottom right.** Owner's: ferns and broad dark leaves at the foot of the basin and the fence. Ours: none; a bucket and a broom stand there.
23. **The basin.** Owner's: a mossy round basin at the bottom right with the bamboo spout pouring into it, close to the eye. Ours: the basin in the middle right, further away, in front of the pond.
