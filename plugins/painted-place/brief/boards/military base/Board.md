# Reference board: a military base

What this folder is: real photographs of army camps and their things from 1914 to 1918, gathered for the request "a military base" (Library of Congress; Bain News Service, Keystone and Underwood stereographs, the American Red Cross collection; all public domain or with no known restrictions; `sources.json` has each picture's source). They show what the place is made of and how it was used. They do not show the look (the look is in `reference/`); the period's photographs are daylight and grey.

## Traps

- These are British, French and American camps mixed; the request is a British one. Use them for materials and use, not for badges.
- The pictures are photographs of daylight; everything in them is lighter and greyer than the look wants.
