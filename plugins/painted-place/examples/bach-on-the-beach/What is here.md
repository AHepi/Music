# The bach on the beach, re-dressed by 0.3.0

What this folder is: the beach house from the first blind test (a fresh builder's shapes for "A bach on the beach in Samoa. Modern day."), dressed again by painted-place 0.3.0, so you can see what the timber, shore and tree details add to a place the plugin was not built for. The shapes are the builder's; nothing in them was changed.

## The pictures

`bach - blind test, re-dressed, up the shore.jpg`, three pictures side by side, all 540 x 960 with the same dials (exposure 0.5, window light 0.5, lamp light 0.7, sky light 1.6, no off-frame amber light):

| Left | Middle | Right |
|---|---|---|
| As the blind test's judges saw it (plugin 0.1.1) | The same shapes dressed by 0.3.0 | 0.3.0, with the camera moved onto the sand looking up the shore (`shore_view_camera.py`) |

What changed between left and middle: the timber is grey weathered boards with one pale paint peeling off them, each board its own shade; the sand is darker, so the fire no longer turns it to snow; the trunks are wet ringed bark. What the right-hand view shows that the blind test's camera hid: the wet band along the waterline with the sky in it, foam lace and a broken swash line on the sand, two broken lines of surf, the wrack line with sticks and driftwood, rocks wet to the tide line.

`close look before the paint change (red read as rust).jpg`: an earlier pass where the paint chosen for this house was a faded red. Under amber light, red paint peeling off grey wood reads as rust; reds and oranges were taken out of the paints.

## The counts (`dressing-report.json`)

From the blind test's camera, with the final code: 25 timber objects given a shade per board, 8 trunks given bark, 571 timber feet seen (224 rot faces, 60 mould faces, 39 flakes and splinters), 3,854 cells of wet sand, 1,529 foam patches and 189 swash patches, 1,167 wrack pieces and 42 modern scraps, 6 driftwood logs, 11 things wet to the tide line, 12 sand drifts, 19 dead palm fronds and 30 coconuts and husks. The dressing took about 40 seconds on a 2-processor computer.

## Traps

- The blind test's camera looks away from the sea, so most of the shore work happens out of frame. The dressing dresses what the camera sees: move the camera, dress again.
- The beach was built level with the sea and cut straight where they meet; the swash line wanders but the edge still reads straight. Slope the sand down under the water.
- The toy-like foreground (a ring, a starburst of rods, a crystal fire) is the builder's shapes: the dressing ages shapes, it does not replace them.
